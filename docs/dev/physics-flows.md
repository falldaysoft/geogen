# Physics props, mechanisms and spawned items

Props the player can push and carry, jointed rigid bodies with motors, and items that spawners, conveyors and turrets move through a scene (flows). Interactions and NPCs are in [gameplay.md](gameplay.md).

## Physics props

`geogen/physics.py`: an asset with `physics: {mass, friction, bounce, carry, spawn, initial}` is one rigid body in the runtime (`meta.physics`, exported in extras with a box `size` / `center` from its geometry).
- `shape: box | sphere | capsule` (default box; sphere radius half the largest side, capsule upright) so a prop can roll; `damping: {linear, angular}` settles it. Test: `tests/test_physics_shapes.py` (a ball rolls down a wedge a box stays on).
- `carry: true`: the player picks it up with E and carries it (E drops it); from further than `reach` when it is on the floor (bending down: eye height + 0.25 m, `player_focus._pick_up_reach`).
- `spawn: "<thing>.<attachment>"` (usually a text param: `params: {from: dropper.spawn_at}`) adds a `spawn` interaction, `stowed | live` (`initial`, default stowed): rules hand it out there, and the runtime stows a live prop that falls out of the world. `resolve_spawns` (end of every `_build_scene`, like `resolve_moves`) records `spawn_at` in the asset frame.
- Places take `detect: [actors, props]`; then they have `occupants` (number, `driven: place`), which the runtime keeps at the count of actors and props inside (`places.place_state`). A floor button is a rule on `{changed: pad.occupants}`.
- Godot: `scripts/props.gd` (`GeogenProps`, `WorldLoader.props`); saves keep live props in `bodies`. See "Physics props" in `runtime/godot/README.md`.
- Interaction motions and targets may name an arrayed part: all its copies.
- `examples/marble_run/` (`--project examples/marble_run -s marble_run`, epic geogen.46): a hopper (a source of four ball items, started by a lever; they appear in a feed tube over its bowl and fall through it) drops 12 cm balls (`shape: sphere` physics items) into a brass half-pipe trough (`ball_track.yaml`, GENERATED by `examples/marble_run/tools/run_path.py`: a ramp, a U-turn, two helix turns round a pole; a `level` sweep, which doesn't roll round the helix). They are physics bodies the whole way (the scene runs physics at 120 ticks, and the balls' damping holds them to about 1.4 m/s): off the end of the trough into `exit_funnel.yaml` (a vortex funnel with a rim wall whose lip curls in), round it and through its neck into a catch bowl with a `sink` place; the HUD counts what's caught. Tests: `tests/test_marble_run.py` (60 s in Godot: nothing leaves the run).
- `examples/test_chamber/` (`--project examples/test_chamber -s test_chamber_01`) is the worked example: a Portal-style course of five chambers (Halcyon Applied Sciences), each a scene whose `test_chamber.yaml` placement's `travel` leads to the next (the asset's `travel_volume` at the exit hall's end; 05 goes back to 01). Elements, all declarative: a dropper (a spawned-items source of `items/weighted_cube.yaml`, one at a time, replaced when lost; its own rule opens the hatch on `emitted`; `initial: stopped` waits for `start`), weighted cube, floor button, sliding door, indicator lines, a chamber sign (`number`, `total`, `progress`), and (geogen.83) `goo_pit.yaml` (placed `on: chamber.floor`, its `cut` opens the slab; a `water` sheet; under the surface a `travel_volume` with `travel: {on: enter, spawn: start}` sends the player back to the start, an asset rule on `{enter: pool}` says so, and `drain` is a sink for cubes: main.gd's `_travel` drops a carried prop first, so a cube goes in with you), `moving_platform.yaml` (`ride`: `near` / `far` states that dwell `then` into each other from load, a translate motion; the deck's `AnimatableBody3D` collider carries the player), `retractable_stairs.yaml` (ten blocks rising out of the floor, one translate motion each; `extend` / `retract`), `pedestal_button.yaml` (`press`: `pressed`, then `released` after `hold` s: momentary or timed), `fizzler.yaml` (a field whose `grill` sink dissolves cubes carried through), and `test_chamber.yaml`'s `exit_height` / `landing` (a raised exit the stairs reach). Scenes with pits say `ground: false` (the runtime's ground plane would stop a fall at y = 0; "Godot runtime" in `docs/dev/runtime.md`). Core textures `panel` (cladding panels with seams, per-panel tone, grime, bolts) and `stripes` (hazard stripes). Tests: `tests/test_test_chamber.py`. Run it with `godot --path runtime/godot -- --generated=$PWD/examples/test_chamber/generated` after `--export-catalogue`.

## Mechanisms

`geogen/mechanism.py` (geogen.86): an asset with `mechanism: {bodies: {...}}` has parts the runtime simulates as separate rigid bodies held by joints, where `physics:` makes the whole asset one prop. Each body lists `parts` (an arrayed part: all its copies; `array: true` makes a body per copy, `<name>_<i>`, its `<part>.<point>` pivot from that copy), `mass`, `friction`, `bounce`, `damping`, and a `joint`: `hinge` (turns about `axis`), `slide` (moves along it) or `pin` (a ball joint), at `pivot` ([x, y, z] in the asset frame, or a part's attachment point / anchor, `hanger.top`), `to` another body (absent: the world; the asset's other parts are static scenery), `limits` (degrees or metres), `motor: {speed, force}`. Parts in no body stay static. Parts of a body collide as convex hulls (`collider: mesh` on one becomes `hull`; a dynamic body can't be concave), so a deck round a mast is a disc that encloses the mast: a body never collides with its own asset's static parts (the frame it is mounted on), nor with the body its joint holds it to, nor with the other copies of its arrayed body hung from the same thing (swing chairs stopped abruptly hooked their chains).
- **Driving motors:** an interaction motion `{body: <name>, drive: {<state>: speed}, free: [states]}` (`layout/interactions.py`, kind `drive`): the motor's target speed per state (deg/s or m/s), eased in and out (a smoothstep) so it reaches its fastest value in the interaction's `duration`; a missing state is 0 (a brake with the motor's `force`); `free` names states the motor is off in (a drop tower's fall); after a free state the motor takes its target at once, so its `force` is how hard it brakes. **To positions** (geogen.88.2): `to: {<state>: <position> | {at, speed}}`, `speed`, `accel` (defaults: the motor's speed, that over `duration`; `Motion.servo`, resolved in `parse_mechanism`, which checks slide targets against `limits`) sends the joint to a position (m along a slide, degrees round a hinge, from the exported pose) the way a ride's controller does: the runtime servo (`mechanisms.gd` `servo`, `_run_servo`) asks for the speed that stops it there, and the state is reached when the body is, so `then` without `after` moves on as it arrives (the drop tower's lift and landing, `lift_platform.yaml`). A state is in at most one of `drive`, `to`, `free`. Timed speeds can't stop a body at a place: the tower's car used to run into the ends of its slide at full speed.
- **Joint damping and swing joints** (geogen.88.2): a joint's `damping` (per second) is a damper on the joint's own motion (a bearing), sized by what the body turns about the pivot, so a swing halves in about 1.4 / damping s; a body's `damping.angular` is air on its spin and hardly touches a pendulum's swing (the Ferris wheel's gondolas used to rock forever). A relative damper also drags: a gondola leans damping x its turn rate against the wheel, so keep it small (0.15 there). `type: swing` turns about `axis` (level) and the level line across it, never about the vertical: a chair on chains flies out and trails behind (the swing ride's chairs lag up to 12 degrees round the mast spinning up: they move out as well as round), with `limits: <deg>` the furthest it swings (80 for the chairs: chains go slack rather than push a chair over its hook). **NPCs moved in one go** (`npc.gd` `_solid_after_move`: off a seat, back from away, a load) get their collider back two physics frames later: Jolt gives a moved kinematic body the speed of the move from where it was, so a collider on at once swept through the seat it left at hundreds of m/s and flung a swing chair over the crown (the old flaky swing test). It poses nothing at build time. Exported as motion `type: drive` with `body` and `free`; the extras `mechanism` carries bodies' parts by exported node name (`exported_mechanism`).
- **Riding:** standing on a body carries the player (platform velocity, as with the test chamber's platform); an affordance with `body: <name>` rides it, and a seated player follows the seat exactly (`player.gd` `_ride`, `GeogenMechanisms.seat_now`). NPCs ride too (`npc.gd`): the runtime keeps a riding affordance's position, yaws and approach where its body is and marks it `boardable` while the body is still and the seat as low as it has been seen; an NPC only chooses a boardable seat, poses at the seat's live position (the option fails if the ride has started while it walked up), follows the seat every physics frame (`_ride`), and when its time is up stays seated until the seat is boardable again. The affordance QA skips seats on bodies (a gondola is wherever the wheel is). For the navmesh a body is an obstacle, never floor: `mechanisms.gd` gives each a static shadow (a box over every shape, at least 2 m tall) that `world_navigation.gd` puts on layer 1 for the bake only (its md5 is in the cache key), so NPC paths go round a ride and reach its seats from their approach points off the body. A seat's approach must be out of the moving parts' sweep: an NPC waiting in a swing chair's path was knocking the chairs about, so the swing ride is fenced (a `fence` with a gap by the lever) and its seats are reached from outside the fence. An arrayed body's joint axis turns with its copy (`array_turn`, build-only meta), so eight swing chairs on `axis: z` get tangential hinges all round. The player is on its own collision layer (`Player.LAYER`, 4) that mechanism bodies don't scan: a kinematic body is immovable to anything that scans it, so a car couldn't lift a rider standing on it; props, NPCs and place areas scan the player's layer. A standing rider still gets in the way of a free-falling car (the rider's contact slows it): rides that fall seat their riders.
- **Polar arrays** (`layout/loader.py` `_expand_arrays`, applied in `_place_part`): `array: {count, turn: deg, axis: y|z|x, about: [x, y, z], upright: true | [x, y, z]}` swings copies round an axis (a carousel's horses, a wheel's spokes); `upright` keeps their rotation, and as a point carries that point round so several arrayed parts stay assembled (a gondola hung from its pin).
- **Godot:** `scripts/mechanisms.gd` (`GeogenMechanisms`, `WorldLoader.mechanisms`): bodies are `RigidBody3D`s beside the asset node in its frame, with the parts moved under them and their exported collider meshes as convex shapes (set up with `props.setup`, before colliders; `exclude_frames` after them adds the collision exceptions); joints are `HingeJoint3D` (local Z the axis; Godot's hinge motor and limits turn the other way from the right-hand rule, `HINGE_SIGN`), `Generic6DOFJoint3D` locked to one linear axis (a slide: `SliderJoint3D` has no motor) or `PinJoint3D`; `drive` / `set_motor` run the motors from `interaction.gd` `_apply`. Bodies aren't saved: a load puts a mechanism back at its exported pose and the restored interaction states run the motors again. `world.bodies` reports every body (centre of mass, `tilt_deg`, speeds, motor). Hinge `limits` are exported but no ride uses them yet.
- **Core assets** that are mechanisms (review scene `-s mechanism_test`, catalogue test group; `tests/test_mechanism.py` drives it in Godot): `swing_gate.yaml` (a leaf on a free hinge with limits: walk into it and it swings open), `seesaw.yaml` (a plank on a limited hinge: a prop on one end tips it), `hanging_chain.yaml` (links pinned `to: previous`, a lantern on the end), `lift_platform.yaml` (a slide with a motor; `ride` goes down → rising → up → lowering, the lever usable from the deck by `reach`) and `windmill.yaml` (sails on a motorised hinge about z, running from load). A rigid body can be pushed by the player (`_push_props` applies to any unfrozen RigidBody3D), props and NPCs; what rules can observe is the interaction's state, not a free body's pose (places don't detect mechanism bodies).
- `examples/amusement_park/` (`--project examples/amusement_park`, geogen.86): a carousel (`carousel.yaml`: deck, poles, horses and canopy one body on a motorised hinge; eight `sit` affordances on the horses ride it; `run` ramps the drive over 6 s), a Ferris wheel (`ferris_wheel.yaml`: the wheel body on a hinge about z, eight `gondola_<i>` bodies on free hinges from the rim, hanging level: open benches for two with knee-high panels, sat on (E) from the south step), a drop tower (`drop_tower.yaml`: a drum round the column with eight outward seats, on a slide joint; `ride` cycles waiting → lifting → holding → dropping (free) → braking → landing; sit from the plinth) and a swing ride (`swing_ride.yaml`: a crown on a hinge, eight chairs on two chains each, hinged across the chain tops so they swing out but keep facing the way they go). Every ride runs cycles by itself (`stopped`/`waiting` dwells `then` into running after `pause` s, running stops after `cycle` s), an operator NPC (`npcs/operator.yaml`, affordance tag `operator`) stands at its lever, and fairgoers ride (seats tagged `ride` advertising `fun`). The scene (`scenes/amusement_park.yaml`) runs physics at 120 ticks. The rest of the fair is existing generation: `fair_stall.yaml` (one parametric stall, `kind` the sign and `colour` the awning, with a `stand` affordance tagged `stall` behind the counter) scattered along the gate avenue and the midway's south-west arc with scatter `facing: left | right | center` (`layout/scatter.py`, geogen.86.2: `along | back | left | right` of a path's direction, `center | outward` of the region, or degrees; `yaw` then jitters it), `npcs/vendor.yaml` (affordance tag `stall`: walks to a stall and stands behind it) and `npcs/fairgoer.yaml` (benches, picnic tables, the zoo's bales) scattered on the paths, `petting_zoo.yaml` (a fenced pen whose `source` makes `items/sheep.yaml`, `goat.yaml`, `pig.yaml` once, walking items with gaits on the pen's looping `track`) and `strength_tester.yaml` (a mechanism: `strike` runs the puck's slide motor for 0.2 s, then `flying` lets it coast up to the bell and fall back). Tests: `tests/test_mechanism.py` (vocabulary; the core review scene in Godot), `tests/test_amusement_park.py` (the carousel in Godot; the other rides in the exhaustive tier), `tests/test_scatter.py` (facing).

## Spawned items

`geogen/flows.py` (epic geogen.45): things the runtime makes while the game runs, moves and takes away. Items have no stable IDs and no rules state; the rules see counts and signals with data.
- **Item** (`kind: item`, `assets/items/*.yaml`): `body` (asset or `{asset, params}`), `mode: physics | belt | walk`, `data` (numbers or text each copy carries), `speed`, `radius`, `lifetime`, `stuck` (s at rest before a physics item goes; give rolling bodies `damping`), `gait: {stride, bob, sway, waddle}` (a walk item's body bobs twice a stride, rocks and swings its heading as it goes; drawn by the runtime, positions unchanged).
- **Source** (asset `source:`): `items` (file or weighted list) or `waves` (a waves file), `rate`, `at` (attachment), `max_alive`, `count`, `initial: running | stopped`, `velocity`, `spread`, `seed`. Parsed into `meta.flows.source`; the composer (`flows.attach_templates`, end of every `_build_scene`) adds a hidden template child per item with `meta.item`. State: `running`, `queued` (rules set them), `emitted`, `alive`, `killed`, `wave` (driven: flows), `restarts`; inputs `start`, `stop`, `emit`, `reset` (adds a restart: the runtime takes its items away, stops it, and its waves and counts start over); signal `emitted {item}` (declared by `state.collect` via `flow_state` / `flow_signals` / `flow_inputs`).
- **Sink** (`places: {hole: {box, sink: true | {accepts: [item names]}}}`): items entering it are taken in; state `received` (driven), signal `received {item, ...item data}` (fields from every template in the scene, `flows.item_fields`, so the export checks `event.<field>` reads).
- **Track** (asset `track:` or scene `tracks:` with optional `surface: {width, material, height}`): `path`, `speed`, `spacing`, `mode: belt | walk | roll`, `loop`, `end: auto | sink | stop | drop`, `accepts`, `damping` (roll). A `roll` track drives each item by gravity ((5/7) g sin(slope) less damping; items spin) and takes physics items too, so items follow a set path down a chute and fall off a `drop` end as bodies (free-rolling balls are plain physics items). Joined by geometry (`flows.network`): a track end within 0.5 m (`JOIN`) of another's start feeds it, a source whose spawn point is that close to a start puts items on it. State `running` (inputs start / stop), `received` (driven); signal `received {item, ...}`. `flows.simulate(root, ticks)` is the deterministic reference (position-ordered stepping, smooth weighted round robin picks) that `flows.gd` matches; `--inspect` check `flows` (`check_network`). Core assets `conveyor.yaml`, `spawner.yaml`, `parcel.yaml`, `rover.yaml`, items `items/parcel.yaml`, `items/rover.yaml`; review scene `-s flow_test` (catalogue test group).
- **Effector** (asset `effector:`): `range`, `rate`, `targets: first | last | nearest | strongest`, `accepts`, `effect: {damage: n} | {take: true}`, `at`, `aim: {yaw, pitch, turn_rate}` (part names, renamed on export), `projectile: {kind: instant | bolt | tracer, speed, color}`, `enabled`. Damage lands as it fires; an item's `data.hp` at 0 destroys it and its source counts `killed` / raises `killed {item, ...}`. State `enabled` (inputs enable / disable), `taken`; signal `took`. Core asset `turret.yaml`.
- **Waves** (`kind: waves`, `assets/waves/*.yaml`: `{auto, waves: [{delay, items: [{item, count, gap}]}]}`): `source: {waves: ...}` (spawner param `waves`) sends them in turn while running; state `wave`, signals `wave_started` / `wave_cleared` / `all_cleared {wave}`; without `auto` the source stops after each wave. `flow_test` has a raid lane with a turret; `simulate` covers waves and effectors and the Godot parity test compares kills, waves and hp.
- **Saves:** a runtime `flows` section in `geogen-save` (live items, source wave phase / timers / pick, effector cooldowns); counts are rules state. A run resumed from a save goes on exactly as it would have (`tests/test_flow_tracks.py`).
- **Runtime** `runtime/godot/scripts/flows.gd` (`WorldLoader.flows`): takes templates out of the world first thing in `setup_root`, runs sources, physics items are `GeogenProps.add_clone` bodies. API `flows.list`. Tests: `tests/test_flows.py`, `tests/test_flow_tracks.py`.
- `examples/tower_defense/` (`--project examples/tower_defense -s tower_defense`, epic geogen.47) is the worked example: an `enemy_gate.yaml` (a waves source; its red strip glows while `running`, by asset rules) sends grunts, runners and brutes (walk items with `gait` and data hp / value / harm; `waves/standard.yaml`, eight waves, `auto: false`) down a scene `tracks:` path (`end: sink`) to `defense_core.yaml` (interaction `damage`: intact | cracked | critical | destroyed, set by watch rules on `world.lives`). `tower_pad.yaml` builds a hidden bolt tower for `cost` gold (`build` with `when: world.gold >= cost`; the pad declares `gold` as world state it uses; its effector's `enabled` is bound to the build); `console_button.yaml` NEXT WAVE / RESET (`gate.reset`, `reset: "[tag=machine.tower]"`); an `overlook.yaml` deck. World state lives / gold / phase, HUD stats and banners. `flows.simulate(..., auto=True)` sends every wave and its result's `through` names what each track took in: the balance test uses them. Tests: `tests/test_tower_defense.py` (check, path and pad coverage, balance, rules on the reference machine, a game in Godot).
- `examples/survival/` (`--project examples/survival -s castaway`, epic geogen.84) is the survival example: an island (`castaway_grounds.yaml`: terrain with levelled pads for the camp, spring, clearing and boulder) where everything is the core vocabulary. Things to find are assets with one interaction and an asset rule that gives the item (`stick_pile`, `loose_stones`, `grass_tuft`, `coconut_palm`: `here | taken`, back after `{hours: n}`; `driftwood`: finite; `berry_bush`: `pick` for the item or `eat` off the bush; `flint_boulder`: `knap` with a stone in hand; `spring`: `drink`; `fishing_spot`: `fish` with a spear, `after: 10` cooldown). Every item has a body asset (a carryable prop) since `kind: item` needs one. `workstone.yaml` is a station: one interaction per recipe, each targeting its own labelled slab (the runtime finds one interaction per aimed part), gated by `when: held(...) >= n`, with `refused` saying what is missing and a rule on the arrival signal doing the `take`s and `give`. `timber_tree.yaml` wraps `primitive: tree` with `chop` (two strikes, `when: has("stone_axe")`, `felled` gives 3 logs, `stump` for `{days: 2}` then standing again); scattered copies each have their own. `campfire.yaml`: `build` (the ring), `fire` (out | lit | fed | embers: feeding passes through `fed` so the burn's clock dwell restarts), `cook`, `eat`; `bind: self.lit` on its `light:`; a `warmth` place whose enter / leave keep `actor.near_fire`. `shelter_site.yaml`: staged interactions whose offers start `pending` and are set to `offer` by the rule of the stage before (frame; walls of logs or woven; roof of planks or thatch; bed), a `sleep` that skips to 06:00, `inside` keeps `actor.sheltered`, `roofed` bound. The scene's needs are actor numbers moved by `every: {hours: 1}` rules (hunger, thirst, energy; health from starving, cold and mending), `asleep` holds energy through a skipped night, and a watch on `health <= 0` is the collapse (sets, takes, skip). HUD stats of the four needs; three quests (`first_night`, `a_roof`, `castaway_life`) whose objectives accept either route. The scene is `atmosphere: {preset: grim, time: "16:00"}`: a low cold sun, haze, drained colour, a night you can see through, and the first night coming on soon after you wake. Tests: `tests/test_survival.py` (check, the loop by both routes and the needs on the reference machine, a Godot playthrough).
- Not built (geogen.48): machines that turn items into others, buffers, splitters, a conveyor primitive with a moving belt, MultiMesh drawing for many items, NPCs taking items.
