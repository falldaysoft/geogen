# Characters

Humanoid bodies, skeletons, clips, hair, clothing, archetypes and clipping QA. `docs/clothing.md` is the clothing design.

## Characters

Humanoid bodies (`generators/humanoid.py`, `assets/characters/humanoid.yaml`): an asset with a `body:` block is a skeleton (`core/skeleton.py`) skinned by ring-lofted chains (`generators/ringloft.py`).
- Chains: torso+neck (into the sculpted head), arm (clavicle to wrist, into the sculpted hand), leg, foot; `mirror: true` adds the exact Right* twin.
- A chain's `rings` are `[bone, t, rx, rz, {offset, power, twist}]`. Chains also take `joint_rings` (bones whose joint gets bend rings), `joint_blend` (per-joint weight blend widths), `weight_shift` (e.g. Head -0.055, so the jaw follows the head) and `bind` (a ring follows other bones, e.g. a lofted mitten the Middle+Ring fingers, keeping 4 influences).
- `max_triangles` decimates the unioned chains (weights kept) before the sculpted parts go on (soles, hands, head, in one union); the body is about 5k-5.7k tris with its head and hands.
- Hands (`generators/hand.py`, `body.hands: {size, wrist: [ry, rz]}`): a sculpted signed-distance left hand along the skeleton's hand bones in the rest pose (a rounded palm, four tapered fingers barely blended into each other, the thumb with its pad, the wrist flattened like the forearm and running back into it), polygonised like the head (3.5 mm grid, within 2.2 mm: ~0.9k triangles, ~0.05 s) and skinned vertex by vertex to the bones it lies nearest, blending over 6 mm across the knuckles, so clips curl its fingers. The right hand is its mirror (`ringloft.mirror_mesh`). The arm chain ends at the wrist at the same radii. Poses merge `&hand_rest`: the thumb folded along the hand and the fingers a little curled (fingertips no longer reach into a thigh the palm rests on).
- Shoulders: the top of the upper-arm tube runs on from the torso's shoulder line (the rest pose is a T, so the rings' side axis is vertical there: `LeftUpperArm -0.05` sits 14 mm down, `LeftShoulder 0.42` 20 mm). Raised, they made a step where the arm met the trunk.
- Shape params: height, build, shoulder_width, hip_width, hips, waist, bust, glutes, limb, head_size, jaw_width, nose, chin, brow, cheeks, lips, ears, neck_length, hand_size, foot_length.
- `presets:` feminine/masculine (`params: {preset: feminine, height: 1.6}`; explicit values win). A list stacks presets in order (`preset: [feminine, curvy]`). Body types stack on a gender preset: slim, athletic, curvy, heavy, petite, tall, stocky, lanky, and `hourglass` (exaggerated, a clothing-fit stress test). They set `build`, `limb_scale` and `belly` rather than the gender presets' limb/waist/hips (curvy and hourglass excepted). `leg_ratio` (hip height / height) moves the skeleton's hips and knees. Asset YAML takes `presets:`/`preset:` and sequential `derived:` expressions in general.
- `body.poses` are skeletal poses (stand, sit, lie, t_pose; body frame), and `body.pose` is the one it's built in; each becomes a static `pose_<name>` clip. The top-level `poses:` are the NPC root transforms: sit drops the hips onto the seat point, lie lays the body along the bed.
- Transition clips (`generator: transition`, `from`/`to` poses): `sit_down`, `stand_up`, `lie_down`, `get_up`. They also move the root: `meta.root` (times, positions, rotations) is its path from the approach point to the pose's root placement, in the frame of where the character ends up (`clips.root_at`). Params: `approach`, `anchor_height`, `plant` (leg IK keeps the feet where they stand), `lean`, `via`/`via_at` (a pose to pass through), `move`/`turn` (the shares of the clip the hips travel and the body turns in). `npc.gd` plays the one matching a pose change and follows the path, blending in what differs from the actual seat (`_advance_transition`).
- `body.clips`: procedural clips (`generators/clips.py`) over the stand pose (walk, idle, and a one-shot `wave`). Work clips (`generator: gesture`: `stir`, `chop`, `wipe`, `write`) are declared, not coded: over one of the body's IK poses (`work_counter`: standing at a worktop of `worktop_height`, 0.9 m, both hands resting on it), each moving hand's target follows `lift` plus a closed `path` of [x, y, z] m offsets gone round `cycles` times a loop plus a `drift` path gone round once, and `bones` add [x, y, z] degree paths (breathing, a nod); keys are evenly spaced and joined by periodic Catmull-Rom (`clips.loop_path`). The pose is solved once and only the moving hands per frame; hands left resting are the clip's `rests`. `Clip.samples` (build only) is how many frames `arms.clear_arms` and `drape.fit` look at: 4 for a gesture clip. `walk` (`speed`, `duty`, `knee_drop`, `bob`, `hip_sway`, `pelvis_yaw`, `chest_yaw`, `arm_swing`, `lift`, `heel_raise`) is an in-place cycle with ground-locked stance feet via two-bone IK (`core/ik.py`), the stride chosen from leg reach, and `meta.speed`/`stride` exported in extras. `idle` is breathing, weight shift and a glance. Clips key every bone plus the Hips translation. Generating a body's 11 clips takes about 0.08 s (FK and IK are array maths, `docs/dev/core.md`); a whole feminine body builds in about 0.6 s in jeans and 1.4 s in a skirt (2026-10-18, geogen.11.5.29).
- Poses take `hips: [x, y, z]` (pelvis offset) and `ik: {LeftFoot|RightFoot|LeftHand|RightHand: {target, pole, flat}, mirror: true}`. Targets are in the body frame, solved per body by `core/ik.solve_limb`, so every preset fits. Humanoid poses: stand, sit and sit_at_table (feet planted for the `seat_height` / `table_height` params), lie, lean_on_sill (`sill_height`), reach_low, look, t_pose. The NPC actions use stand, sit and lie.
- Head (`generators/head.py`, `body.head`): a sculpted signed-distance head (cranium, jaw with its angles below the ears, chin, cheekbones, brow ridge, eye sockets, nose, lips, ears and a neck stub blended in narrowly so the jaw's underside reads, smooth-unioned in the head frame) polygonised by surface nets on a 4 mm grid (evaluated only near the surface), simplified by manifold3d to within 2 mm of the sculpt, relaxed so its triangles are round (`relax`) and shaded with the SDF's normals (`sdf_normals`) and darkened by the SDF's ambient occlusion (`sdf_occlusion`, `HEAD_AO`: sockets, beside the nose, under the lip and jaw, so a flat-lit face still has form): about 1.3k triangles in ~0.08 s. Shape params `nose`, `chin`, `brow`, `cheeks`, `lips`, `ears` (1 = neutral; the gender presets set them) and `jaw_width`. Bodies are 7 heads tall (`head_units`, passed to the skeleton; the sculpt is drawn for 6.5 and scaled by `hs`): at 6.5 the head was big and the chin sat 4 cm over the neck's base, leaving no neck (2026-10-19, geogen.11.5.38.2). The torso chain ends inside it (Neck 0.7) and it is unioned on after the body's decimation (`max_triangles` is the trunk and limbs only), weighted to Head, handing over to Neck below the jaw (`head_weights`). Sockets, nose and lips are placed from the face box with the face texture's `eye_v`, `eye_spacing` and `mouth_v`, so paint and sculpt agree. The painted face: `body.face` holds a `box` plus params for `textures/character.FaceTextureGenerator` (eye_color, lashes, lip_strength, blush, freckles, brows, and `age` (smile lines, crow's feet, under-eye and forehead lines), `stubble`, `smile`); it paints on a `_Canvas` in face units (true proportions: `aspect` is the box's height over width, quantized to 0.05 so faces share textures across heights), 512 texels square (`humanoid.FACE_TEXTURE`), ~0.3 s a face: almond eyes with lid, crease, shaded sclera and an iris with a limbal ring and fibres, brows of hair strokes (no lighter than `BROW_LUMINANCE`: blonde brows in the hair's colour vanished into the skin), lip colour over the sculpted lips. `generators/humanoid.apply_face` gives the front of the head its own material slot with planar 0-1 UVs, the one exception to metric UVs.
- Tints: skin tone (`skin_tone` 0..1 over `SKIN_TONES`) and hair colour (`hair_color` choice) are vertex colours over the shared `skin`/`hair` materials. Godot's `world_loader` turns on `vertex_color_use_as_albedo` for surfaces with COLOR_0.
- Hair (`generators/hair.py`): `body.hair_styles` (buzz, crop, pixie, bob, long, ponytail, bun, afro) are `sculpt:` blocks, signed-distance solids in the head's frame built like the head (surface nets, simplified to 3.5 mm, relaxed, SDF normals) as a separate skinned `hair` node: `cover` grows hair from the scalp above a hairline (heights at the front, temple, above the ear and the nape; `thickness` [crown, sides, back]) that thins over `taper` and dives under the skin, so the hairline is where the surfaces cross (no helmet rim, nothing coplanar); `shapes` (ellipsoids, tapered capsules) are smooth-unioned on (fringe, bob volume, long fall, ponytail, bun), `cuts` carved out (the face), `ridges` add clumps. Skinned to Head (`weights: back`: a long fall goes with UpperChest below the neck), vertex-coloured darker toward the scalp, UVs with strands running down. ~600-1.3k triangles, ~0.15 s. A style without `sculpt` is still built from `chains` + `cut` (`build_hair`, as loose garments are). Choice params are `hair_style`, `hair_color`, `eye_color`; params may declare `choices:` (strings, whole-value `"{name}"` only).
- Clothing (`assets/clothing/*.yaml`, `kind: garment`):
  - Outfits are selected by the `outfit` choice param (`none`, `casual`, `jeans_tee`, `smart`, `shorts_tee`, `casual_f`, `skirt_sweater`, `dress`), with `top_color`/`bottom_color`/`shoe_color` choices from `CLOTH_COLORS`.
  - Tight garments `cover: {chain: [[bone, t], [bone, t]]}` are `ringloft.Span`s: covered rings grow by `thickness`, edges become 2 mm hem ring pairs, and the highest `layer` wins (tops 2 over trousers 1; shoes 3). Tops also cover the thigh tubes' tops so trousers can't show through at the hips.
  - Each garment gets its own material copy so slots survive the union; `split_by_slot` then colours vertices per slot and merges slots per base material (`cloth`, `denim`, `leather`).
  - Tight garments take `ease: <m>` (stiffness): the torso's front, back and side outlines are rolled over by a ball of that radius, so a top spans the hollow under a bust instead of clinging to it (`ringloft._roll`; T-shirt 0.12, sweater 0.22; 0.3 already boxes out the hourglass).
  - Loose garments (`chains:`, e.g. skirt, dress) are shells like hair. `hug_body` moves a shell's walls out to the body under them plus a clearance, measured over the patch of cloth each vertex answers for (half-way to the neighbouring rings and vertices, so a hip that bulges between two rings doesn't show through), so one authored skirt fits every build; the waist rings are authored close to the body and get their room from the body under them, not from a fixed ease (the hip room that used to be added round the hip joints made a slim body's dress jut out at the waist seam). `weights: legs` (`leg_weights`) skins it: Hips at the waist (the start cap above the first key ring takes the body's spine weights), handing over round the hip joints to both thighs, half each with `LEG_SPLIT` (0.25) more of its own side's; `weights: body` is the older `transfer_weights`.
  - Drape (`generators/drape.py`, `docs/clothing.md`): pose correctives for a single-tube loose shell, with no cloth simulation. `drape_frame` re-shapes each ring of the outer wall in its own plane: it clears the convex hull of the posed pelvis/thighs' section by its rest clearance, keeps what looseness the skinned ring has left (none on top of a lap, never longer than the ring was cut), and seated nothing behind the knees is below the seat plane. `fit` drapes every static pose and sampled frames of every clip and bakes them to at most 8 morph targets `drape_<n>` on `clothes` (up to 3 static poses exact, then principal components), keyed by every clip's `morphs["clothes"]` track (weights -1..1). `meta.humanoid.drape` is the report (targets, per clip [largest correction, largest error] in mm); `meta.humanoid.shells` is what `drape.Draper(character, shells).positions(actor, seat)` needs to drape any posed instance unbaked.
  - Arms (`generators/arms.py`, `docs/clothing.md`): poses are authored for a slim body (`stand` hangs the upper arms 8° off the sides) and `clear_arms` adapts them: it runs last in the build and swings each upper arm outward at the shoulder by what this body needs for its forearm and hand to clear the body and the draped shell, in every static pose (exact) and at sampled frames of every clip (spread over the neighbouring frames; a transition's ends take its static poses' swing). Only the UpperArm rotation keys change, and a pose that is clear already is untouched. The built character is left in its `pose` as that pose's clip has it (the swing included), not as the raw pose spec. `meta.humanoid.arms` is {clip: largest swing in degrees}; body option `clear_arms: false` turns it off. A draped skirt is probed through its rings (`_Tube`), not its triangles. It adds about 0.3 s to a build (0.45 s with a skirt). Tests: `tests/test_arms.py`.
  - The waist (geogen.11.5.19): garments declare `part: top | bottom | dress | shoes`. `lap_waist` makes a top and a bottom overlap by `WAIST_OVERLAP` (3 cm) as geometry: a tight bottom's span runs on up under the top's hem, and a top's hem comes down under a loose bottom's waistband (`meta.humanoid.waist_overlap`). The leg chain has `root: Hips`: the tube above the hip joint is pelvis and stays put when the thigh swings (it used to swing out behind a seated body, taking the top's hem with it). A top covers the thigh tubes' tops with `leg: [[LeftUpperLeg, -1.0], {level: torso}]` (level with its torso hem). `smooth_hem` runs after decimation: teeth in the top's waist hem go to the other side, hem vertices are levelled, and the hem takes trunk weights only, so it is one smooth loop that follows the pelvis. `meta.humanoid.garments` lists what is worn with each garment's vertices.
  - Hidden faces (geogen.11.5.24, `docs/clothing.md` "Hidden faces"): `drape.fit` also returns which body vertices are at least `HIDE_DEPTH` inside the draped cloth in every frame and `HIDE_END` from its ends; faces of only those are left out of the drawn `body` (~370-400 on a skirted build, `meta.humanoid.hidden_faces`), vertices kept so indices hold. The whole body stays as `body_closed` (hidden, `meta.build_only`: renders skip it, the export leaves it out) and `humanoid.closed_body(root)` is what seat fitting, `drape.Draper`, `affordance_qa`'s pose clash and clipping's hand check read.
  - A dressed character is about 6.3-7.1k triangles with its hair, up to ~8k with a skirt (~1.1k, dense enough to drape; trunk and limbs `max_triangles: 2400`, the sculpted head ~1.3k, the hands ~0.9k each); a build takes about 0.9 s in trousers, 1.3 s in a skirt (`clear_arms` ~0.3 s, ~0.45 s with a skirt; `drape.fit` ~0.25 s); crowd LODs are geogen.11.5.27.
  - Shoes take a `sole: {height, color, material}`: after decimation, `build_sole` takes the outline of the shoe's bottom (convex hull of the foot's vertices near the floor, 2.5 mm proud), extrudes it from just above the shoe's lowest point to `height` with a chamfered tread edge, in its own colour slot (the shoe's material), skinned from the nearest shoe vertices, mirrored, and unions it on. Sneakers: a white 2 cm sole; boots black 1.8 cm; flats brown 8 mm.
- Archetypes (`src/geogen/characters.py`, `assets/characters/archetypes/*.yaml`, `kind: archetype`):
  - An archetype is a seeded distribution over a body's params: `{random: [lo, hi]}`, `{normal: [mean, sd], clamp}`, `{choice: [...], weights}`.
  - Faces vary too: `adult` draws `age` and `smile`; the young and older archetypes set the age range, and spread `nose`, `chin`, `lips`, `brow`, `cheeks` round their gender preset's values (men draw `stubble`).
  - `extends:` inherits another archetype's params. `variants:` draws correlated bundles, e.g. a preset with a matching hair style; `{archetype: other.yaml}` variants hand the draw to another archetype, as in `townsfolk.yaml`.
  - Built in: `adult` (base), young_woman, young_man, older_woman, older_man, office_worker, hotel_staff, townsfolk.
  - Scenes place a person with `{archetype: characters/archetypes/x.yaml, seed: 3}`. A `scatter:` of an archetype or NPC draws a seed per copy, giving a crowd of different people.
  - NPC bodies take `body: {archetype: ...}`, and the placement `seed` picks the person and seeds the brain.
  - Nodes get `meta.character = {archetype, seed}`. Identical resolved params share one body prototype.
  - Review with `-s archetype_gallery -r out.png --view front_high`. Tests: `tests/test_characters.py`.
- Clipping QA (`geogen/clipping.py`): `measure(character, pose, seat=...)` reports four metrics, plus three at the waist of an outfit with a separate top and bottom, with `report(presets, outfits)` sweeping combinations.
  - `shell_poke`: body vertices covered by the loose shell in stand but outside it in the pose (not ones nearer the hem's opening than the cloth: they left by the opening).
  - `hands`: hand and forearm vertices inside the rest of the body (not their own upper arm: an elbow bent hard folds into it) or the shell.
  - `feet_skin`: bare heels in shoes.
  - `seat`: penetration posed on armchair, chair and sofa via `affordance_qa.pose_actor`.
  - `waist_gap`: skin showing between top and bottom: face samples of bare pelvis, spine and thigh within 12 cm of the top's hem that a loose bottom doesn't cover (any at all when the bottom is tight).
  - `waist_overlap`: `min_mm`, the least the two overlap round the hem in the pose: from the hem to the nearest body surface that shows past a loose bottom (negative when the hem itself shows; capped at 120), or the lapped spans for tight on tight.
  - `hem`: the top's waist hem as a loop: vertices more than 8 mm out of line with their neighbours (above or below at rest, plus what the pose adds), and `ends`.
  - `tests/test_clipping.py::test_tops_overlap_bottoms_in_every_pose` is strict: no gap, 15 mm of overlap and a smooth hem in all eight poses (four combinations in the default tier, 5 presets x 6 outfits with `--all`).
  - `test_nothing_pokes_through_a_skirt_in_any_pose_or_clip` is strict too: no `shell_poke` in any pose or sampled frame of walk, idle and the transitions, and no cloth in a seat (one body in the default tier, five with `--all`; heavy builds are left out: a belly over a lap).
  - `test_review_scenes_pass_the_penetration_check` is geogen.11.5.5's acceptance: everyone placed in `clothing_fit` and `people_gallery`, in stand, sit, sit_at_table and lie and through walk, idle and the four transitions: no `shell_poke`, `hands` or `feet_skin`, four influences a vertex and at most 8 morph targets (two people in the default tier, all 18 with `--all`).
  - `tests/test_clipping.py` guards `tests/data/clipping_baseline.json`; regenerate it with `python tests/test_clipping.py`. Open (geogen.11.5.24, geogen.11.5.25): a heavy build's belly through a skirt, hidden body faces (`docs/clothing.md`).
  - Review scene: `-s seated_fit -r out.png --affordances`.
