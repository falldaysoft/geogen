# Characters

Humanoid bodies, skeletons, clips, hair, clothing, archetypes and clipping QA. `docs/clothing.md` is the clothing design.

## Characters

Humanoid bodies (`generators/humanoid.py`, `assets/characters/humanoid.yaml`): an asset with a `body:` block is a skeleton (`core/skeleton.py`) skinned by ring-lofted chains (`generators/ringloft.py`).
- Chains: torso+neck (into the sculpted head), arm (clavicle to wrist, into the sculpted hand), leg, foot; `mirror: true` adds the exact Right* twin.
- A chain's `rings` are `[bone, t, rx, rz, {offset, power, twist}]`. Chains also take `joint_rings` (bones whose joint gets bend rings), `joint_blend` (per-joint weight blend widths), `weight_shift` (e.g. Head -0.055, so the jaw follows the head) and `bind` (a ring follows other bones, e.g. a lofted mitten the Middle+Ring fingers, keeping 4 influences).
- `max_triangles` decimates the unioned chains (weights kept) before the sculpted parts go on (soles, hands, head, in one union); the body is about 5k-5.7k tris with its head and hands.
- Hands (`generators/hand.py`, `body.hands: {size, wrist: [ry, rz]}`): a sculpted signed-distance left hand along the skeleton's hand bones in the rest pose (a rounded palm, four tapered fingers barely blended into each other, the thumb with its pad, the wrist flattened like the forearm and running back into it), polygonised like the head (3.5 mm grid, within 2.2 mm: ~0.9k triangles, ~0.05 s) and skinned vertex by vertex to the bones it lies nearest, blending over 6 mm across the knuckles, so clips curl its fingers. The right hand is its mirror (`ringloft.mirror_mesh`). The arm chain ends at the wrist at the same radii. Poses merge `&hand_rest`: the thumb folded along the hand and the fingers a little curled (fingertips no longer reach into a thigh the palm rests on).
- Shoulders: the top of the upper-arm tube runs on from the torso's shoulder line (the rest pose is a T, so the rings' side axis is vertical there: `LeftUpperArm -0.05` sits 14 mm down, `LeftShoulder 0.42` 20 mm). Raised, they made a step where the arm met the trunk.
- Shape params: height, build, shoulder_width, hip_width, hips, waist, bust, glutes, limb, head_size, jaw_width, nose, chin, brow, cheeks, lips, ears, neck_length, hand_size, foot_length.
- `presets:` feminine/masculine (`params: {preset: feminine, height: 1.6}`; explicit values win). A list stacks presets in order (`preset: [feminine, curvy]`). Body types stack on a gender preset: slim, athletic, curvy, heavy, petite, tall, stocky, lanky, and `hourglass` (exaggerated, a clothing-fit stress test). They set `build`, `limb_scale` and `belly` rather than the gender presets' limb/waist/hips (curvy and hourglass excepted). `leg_ratio` (hip height / height) moves the skeleton's hips and knees. Asset YAML takes `presets:`/`preset:` and sequential `derived:` expressions in general.
- `body.poses` are skeletal poses (stand, sit, lie, t_pose; body frame), and `body.pose` is the one it's built in; each becomes a static `pose_<name>` clip. The top-level `poses:` are the NPC root transforms: sit drops the hips onto the seat point, lie lays the body along the bed.
- Transition clips (`generator: transition`, `from`/`to` poses): `sit_down`, `stand_up`, `lie_down`, `get_up`. They also move the root: `meta.root` (times, positions, rotations) is its path from the approach point to the pose's root placement, in the frame of where the character ends up (`clips.root_at`). Params: `approach`, `anchor_height`, `plant` (leg IK keeps the feet where they stand), `lean`, `via`/`via_at` (a pose to pass through), `move`/`turn` (the shares of the clip the hips travel and the body turns in). `npc.gd` plays the one matching a pose change and follows the path, blending in what differs from the actual seat (`_advance_transition`).
- `body.clips`: procedural clips (`generators/clips.py`) over the stand pose (walk, idle, and a one-shot `wave`). Work clips (`generator: gesture`: `stir`, `chop`, `wipe`, `write`) are declared, not coded: over one of the body's IK poses (`work_counter`: standing at a worktop of `worktop_height`, 0.9 m, both hands resting on it), each moving hand's target follows `lift` plus a closed `path` of [x, y, z] m offsets gone round `cycles` times a loop plus a `drift` path gone round once, and `bones` add [x, y, z] degree paths (breathing, a nod); keys are evenly spaced and joined by periodic Catmull-Rom (`clips.loop_path`). The pose is solved once and only the moving hands per frame; hands left resting are the clip's `rests`. `Clip.samples` (build only) is how many frames `arms.clear_arms` and `drape.fit` look at: 4 for a gesture clip. `walk` (`speed`, `duty`, `knee_drop`, `bob`, `hip_sway`, `pelvis_yaw`, `chest_yaw`, `arm_swing`, `lift`, `heel_raise`) is an in-place cycle with ground-locked stance feet via two-bone IK (`core/ik.py`), the stride chosen from leg reach, and `meta.speed`/`stride` exported in extras. `idle` is breathing, weight shift and a glance. Clips key every bone plus the Hips translation. Generating a body's 11 clips takes about 0.08 s (FK and IK are array maths, `docs/dev/core.md`); a whole feminine body builds in about 0.6 s in jeans and 1.4 s in a skirt (2026-10-18, geogen.11.5.29).
- Poses take `hips: [x, y, z]` (pelvis offset) and `ik: {LeftFoot|RightFoot|LeftHand|RightHand: {target, pole, flat}, mirror: true}`. Targets are in the body frame, solved per body by `core/ik.solve_limb`, so every preset fits. A target can be relative to a bone instead (`on: LeftUpperLeg`, target `[out, up, along]` m from its head: `along` toward its child, `up` square to that in the sagittal plane), solved after the other targets, so a hand stays on a thigh wherever seat fitting swings it; with `flat` a hand lies along that bone, palm on it (its rest frame read from its finger bones, `Pose._lay`). `contact: true` (build: `humanoid.fit_contacts`, after `fit_seats`) moves it along `up` until the hand rests on what is under it, the skin other than the arms and the clothes draped for the pose, measured with `arms.ArmSpace` so it ends `arms.CLEARANCE` off them and `clear_arms` leaves it be: `sit` says only where on the thigh the palm goes (`thigh * 0.45`), not how high. Transitions hold a hand resting `on:` a bone in either end pose to it as the blend nears that pose (solved toward a point that share of the way to the target, blended in by rotation as far: no snap at full stretch, no elbow flip), and their lean is `sin²`, at rest at both ends. Humanoid poses: stand, sit and sit_at_table (feet planted for the `seat_height` / `table_height` params), lie, lean_on_sill (`sill_height`), reach_low, look, hold_food (standing, the right hand up in front of the chest: the `eat` gesture clip lifts it to the mouth and back twice in 4 s, nodding to meet it; geogen.87.1), t_pose. The NPC actions use stand, sit and lie, and `buy` plays `eat`.
- Head (`generators/head.py`, `body.head`): a sculpted signed-distance head (cranium, jaw with its angles below the ears, chin, cheekbones, brow ridge, eye sockets, nose, lips, ears and a neck stub blended in narrowly so the jaw's underside reads, smooth-unioned in the head frame) polygonised by surface nets on a 4 mm grid (evaluated only near the surface), simplified by manifold3d to within 2 mm of the sculpt, relaxed so its triangles are round (`relax`) and shaded with the SDF's normals (`sdf_normals`) and darkened by the SDF's ambient occlusion (`sdf_occlusion`, `HEAD_AO`: sockets, beside the nose, under the lip and jaw, so a flat-lit face still has form): about 1.3k triangles in ~0.08 s. Shape params `nose`, `chin`, `brow`, `cheeks`, `lips`, `ears` (1 = neutral; the gender presets set them) and `jaw_width`. Bodies are 7 heads tall (`head_units`, passed to the skeleton; the sculpt is drawn for 6.5 and scaled by `hs`): at 6.5 the head was big and the chin sat 4 cm over the neck's base, leaving no neck (2026-10-19, geogen.11.5.38.2). The torso chain ends inside it (Neck 0.7) and it is unioned on after the body's decimation (`max_triangles` is the trunk and limbs only), weighted to Head, handing over to Neck below the jaw (`head_weights`). Sockets, nose and lips are placed from the face box with the face texture's `eye_v`, `eye_spacing` and `mouth_v`, so paint and sculpt agree. The painted face: `body.face` holds a `box` plus params for `textures/character.FaceTextureGenerator` (eye_color, lashes, lip_strength, blush, freckles, brows, and `age` (smile lines, crow's feet, under-eye and forehead lines), `stubble`, `smile`); it paints on a `_Canvas` in face units (true proportions: `aspect` is the box's height over width, quantized to 0.05 so faces share textures across heights), 512 texels square (`humanoid.FACE_TEXTURE`), ~0.3 s a face: almond eyes with lid, crease, shaded sclera and an iris with a limbal ring and fibres, brows of hair strokes (no lighter than `BROW_LUMINANCE`: blonde brows in the hair's colour vanished into the skin), lip colour over the sculpted lips. `generators/humanoid.apply_face` gives the front of the head its own material slot with planar 0-1 UVs, the one exception to metric UVs.
- Tints: skin tone (`skin_tone` 0..1 over `SKIN_TONES`) and hair colour (`hair_color` choice) are vertex colours over the shared `skin`/`hair` materials. Godot's `world_loader` turns on `vertex_color_use_as_albedo` for surfaces with COLOR_0.
- Hair (`generators/hair.py`): `body.hair_styles` (buzz, crop, pixie, bob, long, ponytail, bun, afro) are `sculpt:` blocks, signed-distance solids in the head's frame built like the head (surface nets, simplified to 3.5 mm, relaxed, SDF normals) as a separate skinned `hair` node: `cover` grows hair from the scalp above a hairline (heights at the front, temple, above the ear and the nape; `thickness` [crown, sides, back]) that thins over `taper` and dives under the skin, so the hairline is where the surfaces cross (no helmet rim, nothing coplanar); `shapes` (ellipsoids, tapered capsules) are smooth-unioned on (fringe, bob volume, long fall, ponytail, bun), `cuts` carved out (the face), `ridges` add clumps. Skinned to Head (`weights: back`: a long fall goes with UpperChest below the neck), vertex-coloured darker toward the scalp, UVs with strands running down. ~600-1.3k triangles, ~0.15 s. A style without `sculpt` is still built from `chains` + `cut` (`build_hair`, as loose garments are). Choice params are `hair_style`, `hair_color`, `eye_color`; params may declare `choices:` (strings, whole-value `"{name}"` only).
- Clothing (`assets/clothing/*.yaml`, `kind: garment`):
  - Outfits are selected by the `outfit` choice param (`none`, `casual`, `jeans_tee`, `smart`, `shorts_tee`, `casual_f`, `skirt_sweater`, `dress`), with `top_color`/`bottom_color`/`shoe_color` choices from `CLOTH_COLORS`.
  - Tight garments `cover: {chain: [[bone, t], [bone, t]]}` are `ringloft.Span`s: covered rings grow by `thickness`, edges become 2 mm hem ring pairs, and the highest `layer` wins (tops 2 over trousers 1; shoes 3). Tops also cover the thigh tubes' tops so trousers can't show through at the hips.
  - Each garment gets its own material copy so slots survive the union; `split_by_slot` then colours vertices per slot and merges slots per base material (`cloth`, `denim`, `leather`).
  - Tight garments take `ease: <m>` (stiffness): the torso's front, back and side outlines are rolled over by a ball of that radius, so a top spans the hollow under a bust instead of clinging to it (`ringloft._roll`; T-shirt 0.12, sweater 0.22; 0.3 already boxes out the hourglass).
  - Loose garments (`chains:`, e.g. skirt, dress) are shells like hair. `hug_body` moves a shell's walls out to the body under them plus a clearance, measured over the patch of cloth each vertex answers for (half-way to the neighbouring rings and vertices, so a hip that bulges between two rings doesn't show through), so one authored skirt fits every build (a cap's centre, much nearer the centre line than its ring, stays put: pushed out along its arbitrary direction it could land on a ring vertex and collapse two faces, geogen.91); the waist rings are authored close to the body and get their room from the body under them, not from a fixed ease (the hip room that used to be added round the hip joints made a slim body's dress jut out at the waist seam). `weights: legs` (`leg_weights`) skins it: Hips at the waist (the start cap above the first key ring takes the body's spine weights), handing over round the hip joints to both thighs, half each with `LEG_SPLIT` (0.25) more of its own side's; `weights: body` is the older `transfer_weights`.
  - Drape (`generators/drape.py`, `docs/clothing.md`): pose correctives for a single-tube loose shell, with no cloth simulation. `drape_frame` re-shapes each ring of the outer wall in its own plane: it clears the convex hull of the posed pelvis/thighs' section by its rest clearance, keeps what looseness the skinned ring has left (none on top of a lap, never longer than the ring was cut), and seated nothing behind the knees is below the seat plane. `fit` drapes every static pose and sampled frames of every clip and bakes them to at most 8 morph targets `drape_<n>` on `clothes` (up to 3 static poses exact, then principal components), keyed by every clip's `morphs["clothes"]` track (weights -1..1). `meta.humanoid.drape` is the report (targets, per clip [largest correction, largest error] in mm); `meta.humanoid.shells` is what `drape.Draper(character, shells).positions(actor, seat)` needs to drape any posed instance unbaked.
  - Arms (`generators/arms.py`, `docs/clothing.md`): poses are authored for a slim body (`stand` hangs the upper arms 8° off the sides) and `clear_arms` adapts them: it runs last in the build and swings each upper arm outward at the shoulder by what this body needs for its forearm and hand to clear the body and the draped shell, in every static pose (exact) and at sampled frames of every clip (spread over the neighbouring frames; a transition's ends take its static poses' swing; the samples are read onto the clip's keys along a monotone cubic, level at a one-shot's ends, so an arm settles onto its static pose rather than arriving moving). `ArmSpace` is the obstacle set it probes (the body less the arms, the draped shell as `_Tube`s) in whatever pose its actor is put, which `fit_contacts` shares. Only the UpperArm rotation keys change, and a pose that is clear already is untouched. The built character is left in its `pose` as that pose's clip has it (the swing included), not as the raw pose spec. `meta.humanoid.arms` is {clip: largest swing in degrees}; body option `clear_arms: false` turns it off. A draped skirt is probed through its rings (`_Tube`), not its triangles. It adds about 0.3 s to a build (0.45 s with a skirt). Tests: `tests/test_arms.py`.
  - The waist (geogen.11.5.19): garments declare `part: top | bottom | dress | shoes`. `lap_waist` makes a top and a bottom overlap by `WAIST_OVERLAP` (3 cm) as geometry: a tight bottom's span runs on up under the top's hem, and a top's hem comes down under a loose bottom's waistband (`meta.humanoid.waist_overlap`). The leg chain has `root: Hips`: the tube above the hip joint is pelvis and stays put when the thigh swings (it used to swing out behind a seated body, taking the top's hem with it). A top covers the thigh tubes' tops with `leg: [[LeftUpperLeg, -1.0], {level: torso}]` (level with its torso hem). `smooth_hem` runs after decimation: teeth in the top's waist hem go to the other side, hem vertices are levelled, and the hem takes trunk weights only, so it is one smooth loop that follows the pelvis. `meta.humanoid.garments` lists what is worn with each garment's vertices.
  - Hidden faces (geogen.11.5.24, `docs/clothing.md` "Hidden faces"): `drape.fit` also returns which body vertices are at least `HIDE_DEPTH` inside the draped cloth in every frame and `HIDE_END` from its ends; faces of only those are left out of the drawn `body` (~370-400 on a skirted build, `meta.humanoid.hidden_faces`), vertices kept so indices hold. The whole body stays as `body_closed` (hidden, `meta.build_only`: renders skip it, the export leaves it out) and `humanoid.closed_body(root)` is what seat fitting, `drape.Draper`, `affordance_qa`'s pose clash and clipping's hand check read.
  - A dressed character is about 6.3-7.1k triangles with its hair, up to ~8k with a skirt (~1.1k, dense enough to drape; trunk and limbs `max_triangles: 2400`, the sculpted head ~1.3k, the hands ~0.9k each); a build takes about 0.9 s in trousers, 1.3 s in a skirt (`clear_arms` ~0.3 s, ~0.45 s with a skirt; `drape.fit` ~0.25 s); crowd LODs are geogen.11.5.27.
  - Shoes take a `sole: {height, color, material}`: after decimation, `build_sole` takes the outline of the shoe's bottom (convex hull of the foot's vertices near the floor, 2.5 mm proud), extrudes it from just above the shoe's lowest point to `height` with a chamfered tread edge, in its own colour slot (the shoe's material), skinned from the nearest shoe vertices, mirrored, and unions it on. Sneakers: a white 2 cm sole; boots black 1.8 cm; flats brown 8 mm. Every shoe also covers the end of the leg tube (`leg: [[LeftLowerLeg, 0.9..0.96], [LeftLowerLeg, 1.0]]`): its end cap sits below the ankle joint and is wider than the heel on a heavy build (`lf` scales the leg, `k` the foot), so a shoe on the foot chain alone left bare heel vertices (clipping's `feet_skin`).
- Archetypes (`src/geogen/characters.py`, `assets/characters/archetypes/*.yaml`, `kind: archetype`):
  - An archetype is a seeded distribution over a body's params: `{random: [lo, hi]}`, `{normal: [mean, sd], clamp}`, `{choice: [...], weights}`.
  - Faces vary too: `adult` draws `age` and `smile`; the young and older archetypes set the age range, and spread `nose`, `chin`, `lips`, `brow`, `cheeks` round their gender preset's values (men draw `stubble`).
  - `extends:` inherits another archetype's params. `variants:` draws correlated bundles, e.g. a preset with a matching hair style; `{archetype: other.yaml}` variants hand the draw to another archetype, as in `townsfolk.yaml`.
  - Built in: `adult` (base), young_woman, young_man, older_woman, older_man, office_worker, hotel_staff, townsfolk.
  - Scenes place a person with `{archetype: characters/archetypes/x.yaml, seed: 3}`. A `scatter:` of an archetype or NPC draws a seed per copy, giving a crowd of different people.
  - NPC bodies take `body: {archetype: ...}`, and the placement `seed` picks the person and seeds the brain.
  - Nodes get `meta.character = {archetype, seed}`. Identical resolved params share one body prototype.
  - Review with `-s archetype_gallery -r out.png --view front_high`. Tests: `tests/test_characters.py`.
- Clipping QA (`geogen/clipping.py`): `measure(character, pose, seat=...)` reports four metrics, plus three at the waist of an outfit with a separate top and bottom, with `report(presets, outfits)` sweeping combinations.
  - `shell_poke`: body vertices covered by the loose shell in stand but outside it in the pose (not ones nearer the hem's opening than the cloth: they left by the opening).
  - `hands`: hand and forearm vertices inside the rest of the body (not their own upper arm: an elbow bent hard folds into it) or the shell.
  - `feet_skin`: bare heels in shoes.
  - `seat`: penetration posed on armchair, chair and sofa via `affordance_qa.pose_actor`.
  - `waist_gap`: skin showing between top and bottom: face samples of bare pelvis, spine and thigh within 12 cm of the top's hem that a loose bottom doesn't cover (any at all when the bottom is tight).
  - `waist_overlap`: `min_mm`, the least the two overlap round the hem in the pose: from the hem to the nearest body surface that shows past a loose bottom (negative when the hem itself shows; capped at 120), or the lapped spans for tight on tight.
  - `hem`: the top's waist hem as a loop: vertices more than 8 mm out of line with their neighbours (above or below at rest, plus what the pose adds), and `ends`.
  - `tests/test_clipping.py::test_tops_overlap_bottoms_in_every_pose` is strict: no gap, 15 mm of overlap and a smooth hem in all eight poses (four combinations in the default tier, 5 presets x 6 outfits with `--all`).
  - `test_nothing_pokes_through_a_skirt_in_any_pose_or_clip` is strict too: no `shell_poke` in any pose or sampled frame of walk, idle and the transitions, and no cloth in a seat (one body in the default tier, five with `--all`; heavy builds are left out: a belly over a lap).
  - `test_review_scenes_pass_the_penetration_check` is geogen.11.5.5's acceptance: everyone placed in `clothing_fit` and `people_gallery`, in stand, sit, sit_at_table and lie and through walk, idle and the four transitions: no `shell_poke`, `hands` or `feet_skin`, four influences a vertex and at most 8 morph targets (two people in the default tier, all 18 with `--all`).
  - `tests/test_clipping.py` guards `tests/data/clipping_baseline.json`; regenerate it with `python tests/test_clipping.py`. Open (geogen.11.5.24, geogen.11.5.25): a heavy build's belly through a skirt, hidden body faces (`docs/clothing.md`).
  - Review scene: `-s seated_fit -r out.png --affordances`.

## Animals (geogen.100)

- **A body is not only a person.** `generators/humanoid.build_humanoid` builds any `body:` block: the sculpted head, hands, painted face, hair and outfits are each optional, so chains alone make a closed skinned body. `assets/characters/cat.yaml` is a cat that way, on `assets/skeletons/quadruped.yaml`; nothing in Python knows about cats or four legs.
- **A skeleton's own bones**: `kind: skeleton` with `hierarchy: {bone: parent}` (parents first, the root's parent null) instead of `profile:` (`core/skeleton._own_profile`). Every bone needs a position in `bones:`; Left* bones mirror to Right* with their parents; a bone rests with +Y toward its first child (an end bone: the way its parent points) and X as near the animal's left as that allows. The humanoid keeps Godot's profile, so library clips still bind to it. The quadruped's params are `length` (hips to shoulders), `back` (spine height), `width`, `neck_length`, `head_length`, `tail_length`, `ear_size`; it names its neck and head `Neck` and `Head`, so the runtime's head look (`head_look.gd`) works on it, and it has no hand or foot bones the runtime's arm and foot IK would take (they skip what isn't there).
- **Body keys for animals**: `color:` is a coat instead of a skin tone, `[r, g, b]` over the material or a name from the body's `colors:` (linear values: they are vertex colours, so a mid orange is `[1, 0.42, 0.1]`); `markings: [{chain, color, from?: [bone, t], to?: [bone, t]}]` puts part of a chain, or all of it, in another colour (the cat's eyes are small chains marked whole, its nose the end of the trunk). A marking is a zero-thickness span with its own slot, the mechanism tight garments use; one that would start within a few mm of the chain's last ring is dropped, so put a ring where it starts. Chains whose bones run horizontally set `front: [0, 1, 0]` (rx is then half the width, rz half the height). Material `fur` is matte strands for coats.
- **Motions bone by bone**: clip generator `cycle` (`generators/clips.cycle_clip`): `bones: {Bone: {path, cycles}}` are closed paths of `[x, y, z]` degrees in the body frame over a pose (a negative x swings a leg forward), `hips: {path, cycles}` moves the hips (m), `speed` is the m/s a `walk` suits (the runtime scales playback by it, as for the humanoid's walk). A gait is each leg's path with its keys turned round by its place in the footfall order: the cat's walk is left hind, left front, right hind, right front, a quarter of the loop apart. The biped `walk` / `idle` / `wave` generators stay for people.
- **As an NPC**: `npcs/cat.yaml` (speed 0.45, `wander` and `idle`, `affordance_tags: [animal]` so it takes no seats, attention: look only); a named animal is a character file like a named person (`people/smokey.yaml`). `-s cat` (the body), `-s cat_test` (two cats in a room); `tests/test_animals.py`. Check a body with `-s cat -r out.png --views front,side,iso`, the head with `--frame Head --view front --fov 12`, a gait with `--clip walk --filmstrip 6 --view side`, and in Godot (the offline render is glossier and darker than the runtime).
- **Not yet** (geogen.100.3): where an animal carries a thing (`npc.gd` `_prop_point` still names `RightHand`), other gaits and bodies (hop, fly, perch), jump and climb links between surfaces, sitting and lying poses, behaviour ranges drawn per individual, and animal wants over place qualities.

## Motions (geogen.103)

Procedural interaction animation: the design is the doc "Declarative NPC interaction animation" (epic geogen.103). First slice: `src/geogen/motion.py` (the Python reference rig), `assets/motions/*.yaml`, review tooling in `src/geogen/motion_review.py`.

- **Channels.** The skeleton declares `channels:` (`assets/skeletons/humanoid.yaml`): root, hips, spine, legs, left_hand, right_hand, gaze (shared). Bone globs; a bone is in at most one channel; `end` names the IK end. `Skeleton.channels`. **Hand shapes** (`hand_shapes:` on the skeleton, `Skeleton.hand_shapes`): relaxed, open, flat, press, power, pinch, hook, each the left hand's finger bones in body-frame degrees (z curls a finger, y folds the thumb), mirrored for the right; a shape poses every finger (one it doesn't name is straight). The built body carries its `Skeleton` in `meta.humanoid.rig` (build only).
- **Contacts.** An attachment can say how a body touches it: `grip:` (press, sit, look, ...) and `hands:`. `motion.contact(node, grip, name)` finds the attachment by name or grip, else the first affordance of that type, else the origin. A contact faces the way its object does unless the attachment sets `rotation:` or a `facing:` of its own. An interaction's `touch:` names the attachment a body works it at (validated at load; build only until the runtime port): binding a role to `tv.power` resolves to that attachment. `assets/tv.yaml` has `power_button` (press, the `power` interaction's touch) and `screen` (look).
- **A motion** (`kind: motion`, `MOTION_KEYS` / `BEAT_KEYS`): `roles` bound at start, `params` for `{expr}` in beats (build-time only), `holds` (require 3 / claim 2 / prefer 1 per channel), `posture` change, beats. A beat is a body pose (`pose`, `bones`), `root` (`at` + `offset` + `facing`, or `at` + `posture`), `ik` per end (`target` a role or `role.attachment`, `offset`, `pole`, `mirror`, `grip`: a hand's shape, or a role whose contact's grip names one; without it a hand takes its target's grip when that is a shape, so `press` gets the press hand from the button), `look`, `lean`, `plant: feet`, `over` (else from distance at `REACH_SPEED`), `after` (seconds or `duration`), `emit`, `attach` (`{role: hand}` or `{role: {hand, level: true}}`)/`release`, `interrupt` (yield | finish | hold | drop) with `exit`, `then`. Keys in `NOT_YET` (when, until, do, bend) are rejected until they work.
- **The rig** (`MotionRig(actor, scene)`): `start(motion, **bindings)`, `interrupt(run, how)`, `step()` at a fixed rate. Each step advances beats, arbitrates channel owners (highest strength, then priority, then latest; losing a required channel asks the loser to yield), composes (each owner slerps its channels from where its beat began to the beat pose; root; then owner changes are inertialized on that laid pose, per-channel half-life, before the solve, so IK lands exactly; lean; planted feet by IK; hand IK, bending the spine as little as will do when a hand can't reach and the run owns the spine, found continuously, with what the arm spanned when the beat began counted as in reach and eased to `REACH_SLACK` by its end; head turn for the gaze owner, one look easing in once across beats and following a moved target, with soft yaw/pitch limits; a look that ends, or loses the gaze to another, eases off over `LOOK_OUT` under whatever comes next instead of being inertialized, which sprang the head back at the gaze's 0.08 s half-life), and records `trace` events and per-frame `samples`. Standing root targets stand on the floor the person started on. Over planted feet the root leads the pose (`ROOT_LEAD`): hips back, then down. Hand shapes are laid over the beat pose for each hand with an `ik` entry, so they ease in and out with the beat. A beat that changes posture (its `pose` differs from the posture it began in) plays the body's baked transition clip between the two (`sit_down`: clip meta `from`/`to`) on the channels it holds, other than a hand it reaches with and the gaze, with the root on the clip's `meta.root` path ending at the beat's root target; what the body started with fades out over the beat, and the beat's `lean` is left to the clip. It is taken whole because the build cleared the clip's arms and keyed its drape against that whole movement: mixing its arms with the rig's own blend and lean put forearms 17-100 mm into a skirt. A run whose last beat arrives leaves the root on that beat's target. Drape correctives (`clothes` morph weights) follow the hips owner's pose: from that transition clip's morph track, else lerped between the poses' baked weights (`pose_<name>` clips' morphs); owner changes inertialize them too. A hand's IK solution is blended in from the arm as the beat found it by the beat's progress (no snap to the solver's elbow on the first frame). Not yet: locomotion (the root slides to an approach point) and layers. A reach that bends the spine bends it from the spine as laid that frame (the beat's own bones eased in), not from the beat's whole pose: a beat that both leans and reaches (the squat, a bow) laid its lean whole on its first frame. An arm no motion holds hangs, standing, when the spine bends or leans (`_hang_free_arms`: the upper arm swings so the shoulder-to-hand line is what it is with the posture's spine; a swing, not a solve, so it is no change at all without a bend), rather than swinging out behind with the chest.
- **Take, carry, set down; watch** (geogen.103.4; scenarios `take_carry_set_down`, `watch_tv`). The thing says how it is held: a role with `grip: any` binds its first attachment whose grip a hand holds it by (not `motion.NOT_HELD`: sit, look, press, put, ...); `plate.yaml` and `meal_plate.yaml` have `rim` (pinch), `table.yaml` has `top` (grip `put`, where things are set down). `take` reaches over the grip with the hand open, closes on it (`grip: item`: the grip's hand shape) and `attach`es it `level` (kept as it was against the body's heading, carried by the hand's position, so a plate stays flat whatever the wrist does), then lifts it to a body-frame carry point in front of the right hip. `carry` holds the hand there and only `prefer`s it, so it is started with `take` and has the hand whenever nothing else uses it; `release` of the thing ends every other run bound to it (the carry). `set_down` aims the thing, not the hand (`ik: {End: {target, holding: <role>}}`: the hand goes where the hand-to-thing offset as the beat began puts the thing on the target; entering such a beat with nothing in hand is a `MotionError`), lowers it 2 mm onto the place, releases it there, opens the hand off its grip and goes back. `watch` only `prefer`s the gaze: a `glance` (claims it) takes it and gives it back. Not yet: two-handed things (`hands: both`), carrying as a layer that outlives an action and walking with it (103.5, 103.8), and the free arm hangs with the chest, not under gravity, when the spine bends for a reach (it swings back behind the body).
- **Interruption** (geogen.103.5; scenarios `eat_glance`, `eat_interrupted`, `eat_wave`, `squat_shoved`, `bow_wave`), the design's three kinds. *Overlay*: a motion that takes a channel another run `claim`s (not a shared one: the gaze) pauses that run where it is, its beat's clock stopped (`pause` event); when it has all its channels back it enters its beat's `resume` (else that beat again, from where the body is now; `resume` event), a statechart's history: `eat` resumes from `ready` while reaching or bringing food up, and chews on when it was chewing, so the count of bites holds. Asked to yield while paused, it leaves through its `exit` once the channel comes back. *Yield*: each beat's `interrupt` policy, as before. *Break* (`MotionRig.break_(reaction, **bindings)`, script `break: <motion>`, control API `motion.break`): every motion drops with no exits, what the hands hold is let go of (the runtime's physics; the reference rig drops it straight to the floor, `falling`), and the body inertializes into the reaction motion (`stagger`: body only, `priority: 10`). Motions: `eat` (a seated meal, `self.Head` as the mouth: a ref `self.<Bone>` is that bone where the body has it now, its `offset` in the body's frame), `wave` (an overlay on the right hand), `bow` (spine and right hand, body only), `stagger`. `sit` takes `approach_x`/`approach_z` (where the body stands before and after: nearer a chair at a table). Not yet: a stimulus in a scenario script (the design's `stimulus: thud`; the scripts interrupt by name), and a rise while another motion holds a hand (eat's exit comes first: `eat_interrupted` interrupts sit once the hand is down).
- **Body-only motions** (geogen.103.12; exercises: `squat`, `jumping_jack`): no roles, every target an `[x, y, z]` in the body frame and every distance a share of the body (the skeleton's params: `height`, `leg_ratio`, `arm_ratio`, `shoulder_width`, ... in `{expr}`). A beat's `hips: [x, y, z]` moves the pelvis on top of its pose (planted feet bend the knees to keep up); `loop: {to, times}` repeats the stretch from `to` to that beat `times` times (`{reps}`), then goes on to `then`. `ik: {mirror: true}` mirrors a body-frame target across the body (x negated) as it does poles. An arm reaching forward wants a `pole` (down and out): the rest pole, back, lies along a forward reach and the two sides solve differently.
- **The runtime rig** (`runtime/godot/scripts/motion_rig.gd`, `GeogenMotionRig`): the GDScript port of `MotionRig` for body-only motions (beats, loop, the pose library, body-frame bones, hips, hand shapes, IK to body-frame points with the reach bend and the from-the-start blend, planted feet, lean, gaze at a body-frame point and its fade, arbitration, pausing under an overlay and `resume`, `break_`, free arms hanging, inertialization, interrupts, the trace); `start` refuses roles, `root`, `attach`/`release` (not ported yet), and a posture change does not play a transition clip there yet. Its data is the body's `extras.geogen.rig` (`motion.rig_extras`, set by the humanoid build: bones with their rests in the parent's frame, params, channels, hand shapes, the pose library from the built `pose_<name>` clips), not the engine's import rest (the export leaves bodies posed). `{expr}` resolve at start with Godot's `Expression` over the motion's params and the body's. `motion_player.gd` (`GeogenMotionPlayer`, the skeleton's last modifier) steps it on the physics tick at 30 fps and writes every bone while a motion runs. Motion files reach the runtime as `motions.json` beside the exports (`motion.write_motions`, written with every `--export-godot` and by the `scene_export` fixture). Control API: `motion.start {node, motion, params}`, `motion.stop`, `motion.break {node, reaction?, params?}`, `motion.status` (the rig's bones in world space: a skeleton's own poses don't show a modifier's work). Scene `motion_test` (two people on rugs).
- **Idles, emotes, dances; things on the table** (geogen.103.17): 36 motions in `assets/motions/`, each with `category` (idle | emote | dance | exercise | action | reaction) and a one-line `summary`. Body-only ones build every arm from IK to body-frame points (shares of the body: `height`, `shoulder_width`, `arm_ratio`, ...) and the spine, neck and head from `bones`; the gaze-only ones (`nod`, `shake_head`, `look_around`) go over anything else. A hand's turn is declarative: an `ik` entry's `fingers: [x, y, z]` and `palm: [x, y, z]` are body-frame directions (mirrored with the target); the hand turns in the body's frame from how the beat found it to that turn (`_hand_turn`, a fixed arc, not blended under the forearm, which flipped near half a turn), set under the arm as blended. Hand shapes `fist` and `thumb_up` joined the skeleton's. An arm going from hanging to overhead takes two beats (`lift` first): one blend over more than half a turn snaps where its shortest arc changes side. `drink` and `inspect` take a mug (`mug.yaml`, handle grip `hook`) from the table in front, use it (attached, not level: it tips and turns with the hand) and put it back where it stood: `<role>@start` is where a role's thing was when the run started (reference rig only). `props: {role: {asset, on, at}}` says what a demonstration stages: `motion_review.demo_scenario(name, archetype)` puts a body-only motion on a rug and one with props at a table with them on it (`python -m geogen.motion_review --motion NAME`; `--list [--json]` prints the catalogue). `motion.motion_catalogue()` (also `motions.json`'s `catalogue`) is what the runtime's gallery lists; `body_only` says whether the runtime rig plays it today. Scenarios `emotes`, `idles` (conformance traces), `drink`, `inspect`. Tests hold every idle, emote, dance and reaction to running to its end on two builds with no foot slide and at most a pop.
- **Conformance** (`tests/data/motion/*.json`, `kind: motion_trace`, `motion_review.motion_trace`): a body-only review scenario's rig extras, motions, script, every bone's body-frame position every 5 frames and the event sequence; `godot -- --trace=FILE` replays it (`trace_runner.gd` -> `GeogenMotionRig.run_trace`), 2 mm tolerance (the port matches to float precision). `tests/test_motion.py` fails when a trace is stale: rerun `python tests/data/motion/make_traces.py`.
- **Review** (`python -m geogen.motion_review [NAMES|all] -o DIR [--resolution WxH] [--no-video]`): a `kind: motion_scenario` (`assets/motions/scenarios/`: inline stage scene, `actor` at a contact with offset and facing, `script` of timed `start:` / `interrupt:`, `camera` view and `frame`) runs at 30 fps and renders every frame (static scene once, the skinned person, what the script's motions `attach` and target markers per frame: orange hands, blue gaze). Each run writes `video.mp4` (ffmpeg), `filmstrip.png` (a frame halfway through each beat and at each event), `timeline.svg` (channel owners and beats, events), `trace.json` and `metrics.json`; `index.html` puts every scenario on one page with the previous run's video and numbers beside it. In the cloud wrap it in `xvfb-run -a`.
- **Metrics** (`motion_review.metrics`): `contact_error_mm` (an IK end against its goal on a beat's last frame), `foot_slide_mm` (planted feet), `peak_joint_speed`, `pops` (frames over 900°/s), `settle_s` (after an interruption, until joints are under 60°/s). `peak_joint_accel` (deg/s²: speed swinging frame to frame reads as jerky even when slow), `cloth_mm` (forearm and hand vertices inside a draped shell, probed as `arms.clear_arms` does; negative is clear). `tests/test_motion.py` holds thresholds on the four scenarios.
