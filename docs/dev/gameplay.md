# Gameplay: interactions, NPCs and the world around them

Interactions, NPCs, time of day, emitters, light fixtures and the actions NPCs run. The rest of gameplay has its own notes: [physics-flows.md](physics-flows.md) (physics props, mechanisms, spawned items) and [state-and-quests.md](state-and-quests.md) (conditions, state, wiring, rules, content packages, quests, items, dialogue, HUD). `docs/rules.md` and `docs/quests.md` are the contracts; these notes are the map of the code.

## Interactions

`layout/interactions.py`: asset-level `interactions:` state machines (states with `next`/`then`/`emit`/`prompt`, `motions` that `rotate`/`translate` parts about a `pivot` in the asset frame, per-state `values`, `targets` the player aims at). `SceneNode.interactions` holds them; the loader poses parts for `initial`, `apply_state()` re-poses (absolute). A motion may be `move: {<state>: "<thing>.<attachment>"}` (+ `from: <anchor>`, default bottom_center; usually a text param the scene fills, e.g. `params: {to: plate.center}`): `resolve_moves` (end of every `SceneComposer._build_scene`, nearest scope outward; the outermost scene raises for one still missing) turns it into translate motions in the asset frame, so exports and runtimes need nothing new (geogen.40.2; `tests/test_moves.py`). A motion may be `show: [states]` / `hide: [states]` instead of rotate/translate: it sets `SceneNode.hidden` (renderers and `iter_meshes` skip hidden subtrees; exports still write them, as motion `type: visible`) and the Godot runtime hides the parts and disables their colliders (`examples/sandwich`'s layers). Exported in the asset node's `extras.geogen.interactions` with exported node names (see the schema). States can auto-advance after a dwell (`open: {then: closed, after: 6}`); `lock: {key, locked}` makes an interaction refuse use while locked, and `when: "<condition>"` while the condition is false (see Wiring); interactions may have no motions (the TV's `power`). `reach: <m>` lets an interaction be used from further than the player's reach (eye to hit point; the focus ray looks as far as the longest loaded one): the tower defense's floor pads are always more than 1.5 m below the eye. Floor-plan doors take `lock: <key id>` and `auto_close: <s>` (generated guest doors: `key_room_<n>`, 6 s). Assets declare `affordances:` (`[{type: sit|lie|use|stand, at: <attachment> | [x,y,z], facing: deg, height}]`: chairs, sofa, bench, bed) and `container: {capacity, part}` (wardrobe, nightstand), exported in extras. Part shorthand: `joint: {type: hinge, pivot: left|right|top|bottom|[x,y,z], limits: [0, 100], with: [parts]}` or `{type: slide, axis: z, range: [0, 0.3]}` expands to a closed/open interaction named after the part (hinges default to swinging out toward +Z). `door.yaml` has a `swing`; floor-plan doors (`DoorGenerator`) get one on `leaf_pivot`. Godot: `GeogenInteraction` (`runtime/godot/scripts/interaction.gd`) animates parts, honours locks (`--keys`, L to lock/unlock), `after` dwells, on/off emission, and snapshot/restore (`--save`/`--load`, see "Rules in Godot"); E on furniture sits/lies on its nearest affordance; colliders under moving parts are `AnimatableBody3D`; look at a target within `reach` and press E; `--use=<asset>` / `--use=@aim` + `--wait=S` for headless tests; state arrivals print `interaction event: {...}`.

## NPCs

Everything is data; the Godot side is a generic interpreter (decision recorded on geogen-z2b.1).
- `src/geogen/npc.py`: loads `kind: npc` definitions (`assets/npcs/resident.yaml`: `body` asset, `speed`, `needs` {initial, decay/s}, `preferences` (tag multipliers), `activities` (wander/idle), `scoring` {distance, recency, memory, noise, retry}, `home_margin`, `flags`) and `assets/npcs/actions.yaml` (`kind: npc_actions`): per action a default `approach` offset (anchor frame, x right / z forward), `duration`, and `steps` from a fixed vocabulary: `go_to: approach|anchor|random|nearby|near|far|exit|aside|visit|group`, `face: anchor|portal|player`, `pose: <name>` (+ `at: anchor|approach`), `wait: duration|S|[lo, hi]`, `use: <state>`, `use_item: <id> | "[tag=<tag>]"`, `play: <clip>`, `vanish: true`, with optional `if: <flag>`.
- Affordances (`layout/loader._affordance`, player and NPCs share them) take `action`, `approach` (metres in front or [x, y, z]), `duration`, `advertises` {need: amount}, `tags`, `slots` and `interaction`; each is exported with a resolved floor-level `approach` point. Types: sit, lie, use, stand, look (the player only takes sit/lie).
- Doors declare `portal:` (`door.yaml`: interaction, open/closed states, centre, normal, width, height, depth, clearance) so paths through closed doors run the `pass` action; floor-plan doors with a leaf get the same `meta.portal` (`FloorPlan._door_node`: the leaf's width, clearance just past its swing). `WorldLoader.closed_portal_on` finds the first one a path crosses, for NPCs and the player's `walk_to` alike. Body assets declare `poses:` (root offset/rotation/scale relative to the anchor). The resident's body is the feminine humanoid (`characters/humanoid.yaml`); `characters/capsule.yaml` is the old placeholder. Skeletal bodies also export a static `pose_<name>` clip per skeletal pose.
  - `npc.gd` copies the body's clips (`extras.geogen.clips`) onto its own AnimationPlayer, re-pathing tracks after the body moves under the NPC.
  - It adds a `RESET` animation keyed at the rest pose: Godot drops tracks equal to the exported stand pose, so blends fall back to rest.
  - It cross-fades pose clips (sit/lie), easing the body into place. Standing, it plays `walk` while moving (playback scaled to speed / clip speed) and `idle` when still.
  - The summary reports `clip`, `transitions` and `cost_us` (script time per physics frame); `--simulate` also prints `npc cost:`.
- `attention:` (on by default; `npc.ATTENTION_DEFAULTS`) makes NPCs notice the player: head look-at within a range and cone (`runtime/godot/scripts/head_look.gd`), a greeting when the player presses E on them, and a `step_aside` action (`go_to: aside`) when the player walks straight at a standing NPC. `--greet=NAME[@S]` greets headlessly; the summary reports `greets`, `yields`, `looking`. An NPC that isn't away takes a greeting in any pose and mid-action, so a seated quest giver still gives its quest: only a press within `greet.debounce` (0.5 s) is ignored, and the E prompt finds a seated NPC (no collider) by its body line (`npc.aim_distance`). The response fits the pose: standing, the `greet` action (`face: player`, `play: wave`; the humanoid's `wave` clip is `generators/clips.wave_clip`), then it carries on; seated, it stays put, looks at the greeter for `greet.acknowledge` s and waves from the chair (the wave's right-arm tracks over the sit pose, `GeogenHeadLook.gesture`); lying, nothing. `greet.cooldown` limits the wave only; the summary's `greets` counts greetings and `waves` the waves. A greeting is the rules-layer signal `greeted` from the NPC (`attention.greet.signal`) with the greeter as the actor: `state.collect` declares it for every greetable NPC (`state.npc_signal`), `npc.gd` emits `greeted`, and `WorldLoader` posts it on the event bus. Rules use it to give quests (`{signal: resident.greeted, actor: player}`).
- **Carrying physics props** (geogen.97.1): the steps `pick_up: subject` and `put_down: place`, and `go_to: subject | place`, work on an action context's `subject` (a physics prop's stable ID) and `place` (`{position, approach, yaw?}`); action `put_away` is the four in order. A definition's `reach: {distance, heights: [lo, hi], stand}` and `carry: {mass, size}` (`npc.REACH_DEFAULTS`, `CARRY_DEFAULTS`) are the body's limits: what is further, higher, heavier or bigger fails the step with the reason. `carry.two_hands` (0.2 m): a prop longer than that on its longest side is carried in front of the body below the chest with a hand on each side (`_prop_point`, `_carry_hands`; `GeogenHands.hold(node, {hand: local})` keeps hands on points of a node by the arm IK until `let_go`, and the report's `hands.holding` is each hand's distance from its point); a smaller one hangs from the right hand as before. `GeogenProps.take(uid, holder, point)` / `release(uid, at?)` hold a prop for any actor (frozen, out of collision, set at the actor's hand every physics frame; `taken`, `taken_by`, `holder_of`), beside the player's `pick` / `drop`, which takes a prop out of an NPC's hand; released, the prop is a free body just above the place and settles by the physics. An NPC lets go when its action fails, when it goes away, and on a load. `npc.gd` `stand_for(point)` is where to stand to reach a point: the navmesh of the floor under it (asked from hand height below the point: there is navmesh on roofs). The report has `prop`, `picked_up`, `put_down`; `world.bodies` has `holder`. The control API's `npcs.do {npc, action, subject?, place?}` runs an action with that context. Scene `-s wants_test`; `tests/test_wants_runtime.py`.
- **A prop's own affordance** (geogen.104): an affordance on a carryable physics prop (`book.yaml`'s `read`) goes where the prop goes. `world_loader.gd` marks it with the prop's stable ID (`prop`); in `options()` it is offered only while the prop can be taken (`props.can_take`) and from where this body can reach it (`stand_for` the prop's centre, which is the approach; the anchor is the prop's centre), and its action's context gets the prop as `subject` and where it lies as `place` (`_where_it_lies`: the middle of its box's underside, its heading, and where to stand), so `put_down: place` puts it back as it was. Action `read` (`npcs/actions.yaml`): go to where it stands to reach the book (the option's approach), pick it up, `find: [sit, lie]` with `prefer: {quality.soft: 0.25}` (step `find`, `npc.FIND_TYPES`, `npc.gd` `_find_rest`: the nearest free affordance of those types this NPC may use, in its home, not another's, not reserved, nothing on it, its own things counting half as far, a `prefer` tag that share, a seeded +-40%; it becomes the context's anchor, approach and heading, is reserved until the option ends, and `pose: found` takes its pose; nothing free: the pose steps do nothing and it reads where it stands), go there and sit or lie, `use: open` (the book's own `book` interaction: each half, board and pages, swings a quarter turn about the spine), read, `use: closed`, stand, go back and put it down. A prop's `place` has no fixed `approach`: `go_to: place` works out where to stand from wherever the NPC is then. A thing carried in both hands is held where it was against the chest when it was picked up (`_carry_on`, in the Chest bone's frame), so it goes onto a lap seated and over the chest lying down. `_fail` stands a seated or lying body up (it had no `approach` to stand at and left it seated, walking on). `bookshelf.yaml` has a surface per shelf (`shelf_0` the plinth's top to `shelf_3`) beside its fixed rows; the cottage has six loose books on `shelf_1` and `shelf_2`. Tests: `tests/test_books.py`, `tests/test_books_runtime.py`.
- **What is carried shows, and changes with what is done with it** (geogen.105): an item with `held` is drawn in the NPC's hand while carried (groceries, the dirty plate; `_hold`); the step `set_out: <item>` puts a copy of the carried item's body on the top in front of the seat (looking down from 0.7 m above the seat, 0.3-0.7 m ahead, for the first thing above it) until the option ends or the item is used up, and not in the hand meanwhile (`_set_out_item`, `_clear_set_out`); `show: {<item>: <state>}` gives that body, in hand or set out, a state of its own interaction: its `visible` motions show and hide parts (`npc.gd` `show_state`, from the exported extras), remembered while the item is carried. `meal_plate.yaml`'s `food` interaction is full, half, empty; the chair's `eat` use names its own action (`uses` take `action` now), `eat`: sit at the table, set the meal out, eat it down, and the use's `takes: meal, gives: dirty_plate` leaves the dirty plate in hand. A full meal is not drawn in the hand (a hanging arm can't carry it flat). Tests: `tests/test_meals.py`, `tests/test_meals_runtime.py`.
- **Wants** (geogen.97; `npc.WANT_KEYS`, `runtime/godot/scripts/npc_wants.gd`): a definition's `wants: {name: {of, when, action, place, weight, reluctance, forget, advertises}}` are conditions on the world the NPC puts right with whatever is there; no code names a behaviour. `of: prop` + `when: {resting_on: floor}` makes every carryable physics prop on a floor in its home region one more option in `options()` (id `want/<name>/<prop uid>`, utility `weight` + what `advertises` adds, no recency penalty: what fell again is a new problem), reserved per subject (`subject/<uid>`). Choosing it, `GeogenWants.begin` picks a place: `GeogenPlaceSearch.spots` (`places_search.gd`) casts down a 20 cm grid of columns over the home region, up to four levels each, against colliders, and keeps a level that is level, isn't floor, holds the four corners of the prop's box and has room for it; `stand_for` must find somewhere to stand in reach. Nothing is authored as a place: a table top, shelves and a chair seat all turn up. Spots are scored by `place` (`npc.PLACE_DEFAULTS`: the thing's tags x `prefer`, less `distance` per metre, less `off_height` per metre from `height` above the feet, less `tried` for a spot within 0.3 m of one already used for that prop, plus `noise` x the NPC's seeded random), and the best goes to the action as `place` (`put_away`). No spot: the option fails ("nowhere to put it") and waits `scoring.retry`. "Floor" is by height: level (8 cm) with the floor under the nearest navmesh within 1 m, because the navmesh stops a body's width short of walls. `npcs/resident.yaml` has `tidy` (prefers tables, then bookshelves). The report has `wants` (done); the trace has `want` events (subject, place, score, how many spots, reluctance); `npcs.places {npc, want, prop}` lists the scored spots and what the prop rests on. **Reluctance** (geogen.97.4): every pick-up of a prop by an NPC, for a want or not and whether or not the put-down then works, is a handling (`npc._handled`, `GeogenWants.note_handled`); a handling fades linearly over the want's `forget` s (600), and each takes `reluctance` (0.1; the resident 0.15) off the option's utility. With none of the `weight` left the option isn't offered, which is not a failure: wants that undo each other, a thing that won't stay put and a player who keeps knocking it down all end. Handlings aren't saved. **Showing intent**: a step's `look: subject | place | none` (`npc.LOOK_TARGETS`, any step) turns the head to the action's subject or place from then on, until a later step says otherwise or the action ends; `put_away` looks at the subject as it goes for it and glances at the place (`wait` + `look: place`) before setting off. It is `_intent_point()` in `_attend`, through the same `GeogenHeadLook` and neck limits as looking at the player; someone who just greeted it comes first. `stand_for` keeps a candidate spot `STAND_SLACK` (0.2 m) inside the reach, because a walk ends a little off its target and a spot only just in reach then failed the put-down. The report has `want: {seen, left, doing, handled}` (`GeogenWants.report`: the want options at the last decision with scores, the ones it has had enough of, the `want` trace event of the one in hand, handlings per prop), `looking_at` and `head_turn` (deg). **Qualities of places** (geogen.97.6; `npc.PLACE_QUALITIES`, `GeogenPlaceSearch.quality`): a want's `place.quality: {name: weight}` (or a name, or a list: weight 1 each) adds weight x how much a spot has of that quality, 0..1, so a want can ask for what a place is like instead of what it is. Worked out against the colliders: `high` (height above the asker's floor, 1 at 2 m), `enclosed` (the share of eight ways sideways and one up blocked within 0.6 m: between shelves, under a table), `hidden` (of the places to stand 2 m around it, on the navmesh, the share with no line from eye height), `warm` (0.7 where a ray toward the sun is clear and the sun is up, `clock.sun_height() > 0.1`; or on or near a thing tagged `quality.warm`, fading to nothing 1.5 m away). Authored: a thing tagged `quality.<name>` gives every spot on it 1 for that name (`quality.soft` on the sofa, bed and armchair; a placement's `tags:` can add one, as the lit lamp in `wants_places` is warm), which is also how a new quality is added without code. Only the qualities a want weighs are worked out when it chooses; `npcs.places` reports them all. `people/tomas.yaml` (tidy by `{high: 1.0, enclosed: 0.5}`, `off_height: 0`) in `-s wants_places`; `tests/test_wants_places_runtime.py`. **Wants about things** (geogen.97.5): `of` may be a reference to things with an interaction (`"[tag=switch.light]"`, `"[tag=door]"`, `hall/switch`) instead of `prop`, with `when: {state, room: empty}`, `to: <state>`, an optional `interaction:` (default: the thing's only one) and an action such as `set` (`go_to: approach`, `face: anchor`, `use: goal`). The reference resolves in the scene the NPC is placed in (`npc.resolve_wants` from the composer's placement extras: the nodes it names that have that interaction, so a door but not its leaf; both states must exist), is kept as nodes in `meta.npc_want_subjects` (build-only) and exported as `npc.wants.<name>.subjects: [{subject, interaction}]` (`npc.want_extras`); a selector that names nothing leaves the want idle in that scene. In the runtime (`GeogenWants._thing_options`) a subject is a problem while its interaction rests in `when.state` and isn't locked; `room: empty` asks that no character, the NPC included, is in the room volume of the point one stands at to work it (so a light isn't put out on oneself, and it is put out once everyone has left). The option's context is `npc.gd` `_goal_context`, shared with NPC quest goals; for a door (a portal) that is now the portal's own standing point on the side the leaf swings away from, with `portal` and `side` so the hand takes the handle, not a point in front of the leaf wherever it has swung to. Working a thing for a want is a handling (`<uid>#<interaction>`), so reluctance ends a light the player keeps putting on. `people/hana.yaml` (the resident role plus `lights_out` and `doors_shut`) in `-s wants_house` (a scene folder with its two-room `flat.yaml`); `tests/test_wants_state_runtime.py`. **A seat with something on it** (geogen.97.7): an affordance whose action poses the body on its anchor (a `pose` step other than `stand`, `at: anchor`: sit, lie) isn't offered while a loose physics prop rests on that thing within 0.35 m of the anchor (`npc.gd` `_something_on`, by `GeogenPlaceSearch.resting_on`: a can on the table a chair is tucked under doesn't count); the NPC sits somewhere else, and the seat is a seat again once the thing is gone. Clearing it first would be a want of its own. Tests: `tests/test_wants_runtime.py`, `tests/test_npc.py` (data).
- **Perception** (geogen.99.1; `npc.SENSES_DEFAULTS`, `REACT_KEYS`, `assets/npcs/stimuli.yaml`): opt-in per definition, so an NPC without `senses:` notices nothing and connects to nothing. A stimulus is data: a bus event type (`impact`, `changed`), optionally only from subjects carrying a tag (`of: door`), with a `loudness` (a share of the hearer's `hearing.range`). `impact` is new on the bus (runtime-only, `props.gd` `_impact`): a free prop that loses 40% of a speed over 0.8 m/s in one frame hit something; `data.at`, `data.strength` (speed / 4 m/s), and as actor whoever had it last (`take` / `pick` record it), so a put-down, which settles from 1.5 cm, is no impact, and nobody notices what they did themselves. A definition's `senses: {hearing: {range, muffled}, sight: {range, cone}}` (`true` for the defaults) and `reacts: {stimulus: {look, interrupt}}` are exported with the stimulus inlined (`npc.reacts.<name>`: event, of, loudness, look, interrupt). `npc.gd` `_on_event` listens only when there are reacts: heard within range x loudness x strength (x `muffled` when a ray from the head to the point is blocked), or seen within `sight.range` and the cone with a clear line. Noticing turns the head there for `look` s (below a greeter, above a step's `look:`), and with `interrupt: true` cuts the current option short (`_cut_short`): only an affordance or an activity whose action has `interruptible: true` in `actions.yaml` (sit, sit_at_table, look, stand, wander, idle), with empty hands, off a ride, outside a routine hold and with no reaction or door on top; seated or lying it skips to the action's `pose: stand` step, on its feet the action ends. The option counts as done (no failure, no retry), so the next decision takes up the want about what happened: a thing dropped in the room is seen to in seconds, not when the sit runs out. The report has `noticed`, `notice` (`{stimulus, subject, at, by: heard | seen, clock}`) and `cut_short`; the trace has `notice` and `cut_short`. `people/hana.yaml` reacts to `thud` (interrupt) and `door` (look); `tests/test_perception_runtime.py`. **Memory** (geogen.99.2; `npc.MEMORY_DEFAULTS`, `runtime/godot/scripts/npc_memory.gd` `GeogenMemory`): with `memory: {forget, every, search}` (`true`: 300 s, 0.5 s, `look_for`; needs senses) an NPC's wants about props are about what it knows, not about every prop in its home region. `npc._memory` is prop uid -> `{at, clock, by: seen | heard, resting: floor | thing | held | ""}`. Every `every` s it looks round (`GeogenMemory.step`): each prop whose middle it sees (`npc.gd` `_sees`: in `sight.range` and the cone, nothing but the prop in the way) or holds is remembered afresh with what it rests on (`""` while it moves); a remembered spot it can see where the thing no longer is it forgets (trace `forget`); an entry not refreshed for `forget` s goes. A stimulus it notices whose subject is a prop (`_on_event` -> `GeogenMemory.noticed`) is remembered as seen if it can see the thing then, else as heard at the stimulus's point with `resting: ""`. In `GeogenWants.options` a memory NPC's prop options are its remembered props, at the remembered point, offered when the remembered `resting` is the want's `when.resting_on` or not known; `begin` runs the `search` action instead of the want's when the entry is only heard or the prop is more than `MOVED` (0.3 m) from it (the option gets `search: true`, no `advertises`, report `searches`, want trace `search`), with the remembered point as anchor and `stand_for` it (or the nearest navmesh) as approach; when the search ends (`GeogenMemory.searched`) what it saw there is in memory, and an entry it still can't act on is dropped, so the next decision puts the thing away or forgets it. Thing wants (`of` a reference) still read the state of their subjects directly. Memory isn't saved. Report: `memory` (`{uid: {at, by, resting, age}}`), `searches`; trace `remember`, `forget`. `people/hana.yaml` has `memory: true`: a can dropped behind a shut door is no want of hers until she sees it, one she only heard fall beside her chair she goes to look at first. Not yet: more stimuli and reactions that run an action, memory of things' states (geogen.99.3).
- **What affordances need and do** (geogen.101.1, the dollhouse epic geogen.101; `loader._affordance_chain`, `inventory.resolve_items`): an affordance's `requires: {holding: <item> | "[tag=t]" | [any of these], state: {<interaction>: <state>} | <state of its own interaction>}`, `takes: <item | tag | list>` (one of them, used up) and `gives: <item> | [items] | {item: count}` say what doing it needs and what it does to what the actor carries; tags resolve to item ids at build time (every `items/*.yaml` of the content roots with that tag), and the items are declared in the package like any a rule names (`quests.collect`). In the runtime (`npc.gd` `_meets`, `_carry_out`) an affordance whose `requires` doesn't hold isn't an option, and a finished one takes then gives through `world.rules.give` (trace `carry`). These are the edges the planner (geogen.101.3/101.4) chains through: a fridge gives raw food, a stove takes it and gives a meal, a table takes the meal. Until then an NPC only takes such a step when it happens to meet it.
- **Plans** (the dollhouse, geogen.101.2–101.5; `geogen/planner.py`, `runtime/godot/scripts/npc_planner.gd` `GeogenPlanner`): a definition with `planner: true | {depth, patience, nodes}` (`npc.PLANNER_DEFAULTS`) decides by chains of affordances instead of one at a time. The planner is runtime-neutral, like the rules machine: `planner.py` is the reference, `npc_planner.gd` its port, and both must pass the traces in `tests/data/planner/` (hand-written expectations in `make_traces.py` there; `tests/test_planner.py`, `tests/test_planner_godot.py` through `--trace=FILE`, which `trace_runner.gd` hands to the planner when the file has a `world`). Its input is a plain snapshot (documented at the top of `planner.py`): affordances (`id`, `at`, `duration`, `advertises`, `requires {holding, state}`, `takes`, `gives`, `sets`, `weight`, `worth`, `reserved`), `states`, the actor (`at`, `speed`, `holding`, `needs`, `urgency`) and `limits`. Per affordance that would fill a need (or has a `worth`) it searches backward, A* over (what is still required, where the chain starts), a step being an affordance that provides something still required; a chain is complete when the rest holds as the world stands. Cost is straight-line walking at the actor's speed plus durations, in whole milliseconds; ties go to the chain whose step ids sort first (deterministic, so the port matches exactly). The goal is the chain with the best (what it adds to the needs x urgency x the liking for each step) / (time + `patience`). In `npc.gd` (`_next_planned`, `_snapshot`, `_drop_plan`): the options (`options(true, true)`: with `chains`, affordances whose `requires` don't hold yet are kept) become the snapshot, activities with no place and wants with their utility as `worth`; `liking` is every multiplier the old scoring applies (preferences, owns, group, routine) less recency. A plan reserves every affordance in it; each decision takes its next step if that is still an option and its `requires` hold, else the plan is dropped (trace `replan`) and made again; a step that finishes without what it `takes` fails ("nothing left of what it takes"). With no plan to be had it takes the best legacy option it can do as things are. Report: `plan` ({goal, steps, index, cost, score}), `plans`, `plans_done`, `replans`; trace `plan`, `replan`. Not yet: `sets` is in the planner and the snapshot but nothing exports it (an affordance that changes a state, the TV); wants keep their own place search; social (101.6).
- **Working together** (geogen.110.1): an affordance's `sets: {<interaction>: <state>}` is what it leaves this asset's interactions in when it is done (`loader._affordance_chain`, `npc.gd` `_carry_out`), and the planner chains through it like `requires.state`. A definition's `planner: {hands: n}` is the most it carries at once: the planner refuses chains that carry more, and its plan names a `run`, the steps up to the one that leaves the hands empty, which is all the NPC claims (`_next_planned`), planning again when the run is done, so a job stays open to others. What another NPC's claimed steps will set is the snapshot's `promised` (`npc.gd` `promises`): it counts as coming, so two people take different parts of one job; a run that needs a state not yet so is reported in `waits`, and the NPC does something else meanwhile. An affordance's `requires: {near: {tag, state, within}}` is about another thing: the nearest with that tag in front of the place (`world_loader.gd` `near_thing`), so the sofa's `watch` needs the TV on and the planner chains through the TV's own switching-on; nobody takes an affordance whose `sets` would undo a state another's plan counts on (`npc.gd` `relies_on`). A definition's `skips: [tags]` leaves affordances with those tags to someone else. Traces `tests/data/planner/hands.json`, `shared_job.json`. A use may name its own `interaction` (each ornament spot at one standing place), and action `hang_ornament` reaches it with motion `place_ornament`. `-s christmas_eve` is the worked example: `tree_decorated` is the only need that asks for anything, and `christmas_tree.yaml`'s affordances say the rest; `tests/test_christmas_runtime.py`.
- **Needs** (geogen.101.2; `npc.NEED_KEYS`): `urgent` + `boost` make a need's shortfall below the threshold count `boost` times more (`planner.urgency`, used by the old scoring too: without a curve it is `1 - level` as before). For NPCs that plan, needs fill over an option's duration (`npc.gd` `_fill_over`; the others get it all when the option is done, as before: their decay rates are tuned to that, e.g. a diner's rest falls faster than a seat would fill it; the top action's `wait: duration` / `loop: duration`), the rest when it is done (cut short by something noticed it still counts as done, as before); a failed one keeps what its time gave, where it used to give nothing. `at_zero: {action, recover}` is what a need running out does: `_run_out` runs the action where the NPC stands, before anything else (`collapse`: asleep on the floor), giving `recover` back; report `ran_out`. `mood` in the report is the mean of the needs. The control API's `npcs.needs {npc, needs}` sets them (tests).
- **Whose affordances** (`npc.gd` `_for_me`): an NPC that doesn't plan isn't offered an affordance that only `gives` (a link in a chain), and nobody without one of the needs an affordance advertises is offered it (a shower, to someone with no `hygiene`); so household affordances on shared assets don't change what residents and hotel guests do.
- **Household content** (geogen.101.5): `fridge.yaml` gives `raw_food`; `kitchen_range.yaml` (its cook affordance's use `meal`) and `microwave.yaml` take it and give a `meal`; `chair.yaml` (its seat's use `eat`) takes the meal, advertises `hunger` and gives a `dirty_plate`; `kitchen_sink.yaml` takes that (`order`); `shower.yaml` and `vanity.yaml` advertise `hygiene`; beds, sofas and shelves already advertise `rest` and `curiosity`. Action `attend` stands at a thing while it works. `npcs/householder.yaml` (hunger, rest, hygiene, curiosity, order; `planner:`) in `-s household_test` and `-s household_microwave` (the same kitchen with a microwave: the plan goes through it instead); `tests/test_household_runtime.py`. **Uses** (`loader.AFFORDANCE_USE_KEYS`): an affordance's `uses: {name: {requires, takes, gives, advertises, duration, tags}}` are other things done at the same place; `world_loader.gd` makes each an affordance of its own for NPCs (`affordance_uses`, id `<node name>#<index>.<name>`, e.g. `chair#0.eat`) that shares the place, the action and the slots (`_shares`: reservations go by the base id, so nobody sits where someone is eating). The player, QA and `--affordances` see one affordance per place. A second affordance at the same seat is not the way: it doubled the seat for QA and let two NPCs take it. Any export containing a chair, range, fridge, microwave or sink declares the items they name and carries their bodies hidden under `item_icons` (about 800 triangles once per export, kept outside the tree by `world_loader.gd` and freed on exit). Held waits (a routine block's `hold`: the bed until morning) don't fill gradually: all of it comes at the end, as before.
- **Company** (geogen.101.6; `npc._social`, `OFFER_KEYS`, `RELATION_DEFAULTS`): a definition's `offers: {name: {action, answer, advertises, duration, weight}}` is what other NPCs may do with this one, an affordance a person carries. To an NPC that has this one within its home, each offer is an option `with/<stable ID>/<name>` (`npc.gd` `_people_options`), scored and planned like any other (the planner sees an entry with `at`, `duration`, `advertises`: `planner.py` and its traces are unchanged). It is an option only while the other is `free_for_company()` (here, not in a routine `hold`, with nobody already, and seated or on its feet with no plan under way), outside this NPC's own `only:` blocks, and once a need it advertises has room for half of what it gives (`COMPANY_ROOM`: nobody is bothered for a top-up). Taking it up holds both for each other (`_partner`, `_take_up` / `_part`; released when the option ends however it ends). The one who chose it runs `action` (`chat`: the new step targets `go_to: partner`, a pace short of them on the side it comes from, `face: partner`, `look: partner`, then `wait: duration`); the other, on its feet, runs `answer` on top of what it was doing as a reaction (`chat_reply`: `wait: partner`, turned to them until they are done or 90 s pass), and seated stays seated and turns its head (a reaction would restart its seat's wait: meals would get long again). Both get `advertises`, the other its share as the first's fills (`_share`). **Relations**: `relations: {household, stranger, grow, friend}`; a placement's (or character file's) `household: <name>` is exported as `npc.household`, and `knows(other)` is `household` for the same one, `stranger` otherwise, plus `grow` per offer seen through together (runtime `relations`, in the report and in saves). An offer's score is multiplied by 0.5 + how well it knows them; a seat beside someone it knows `friend` or better gets the groupmate bonus (`_beside_friend`: eating together). The report has `with`, `joined`, `relations`. `npcs/householder.yaml` has a `social` need and offers `chat`. Scene `-s household_pair`; `tests/test_household_runtime.py`. Not done: things two do that need a place (a game at a table), and people from different homes meeting (homes don't overlap in the dollhouse).
- **A life** (geogen.111; `npc._life`, `LIFE_KEYS`, `CHAPTER_KEYS`, `runtime/godot/scripts/npc_life.gd` `GeogenLife`): what stays changed about a person, where needs only fill and empty. A definition's `life: {practice: {name: {from: [tags], per_use}}, chapters: [{name, story, hopes, pursues, until, becomes, tends}]}`. Finishing an option with one of a practice's tags adds `per_use` (`GeogenLife.did`, from `_finish_option`), at most `DAILY_CAP` (3) times a day, so a skill takes days. When everything in a chapter's `until` holds (`practice` levels, `knows` a person by character file, `world`: an interaction of that name shows a state) the next chapter starts: its `becomes` changes the numbers decisions already read (`preferences`, need keys, `home.rects` joined to the home polygon), every interaction named in `tends` goes to that state, and `story` is written with the day and time. `_become` rebuilds the definition from the numbers the person started with up to the current chapter, so a load into an earlier chapter is right too. `pursues: {tag: worth}` adds to an option's `base` (the planner's `worth`) until that practice is done for the day: it is what gives someone something to do beyond topping up needs. **Days skipped** (`days_passed`, from the rules' `skip` effect and `time.skip`) aren't simulated: each one counts what they did on their last day again, capped the same way, and spending time with someone counts toward knowing them. `life` is in the NPC's save and in `characters` (it goes with a named person between scenes). Report: `life` ({chapter, index, hopes, practice, story}); trace `chapter`. `GeogenHudLife` (`hud_life.gd`) shows a card on a greeting or a new chapter: what is new in their story since it was last shown, and what they hope for. `examples/dollhouse` scene `walters_week`: Walter's seed tin is on a shelf above his reach (`_prop_affordance` offers nothing out of reach), and lifting it down is the only help there is; he sows, the beds come up, the flowers bring Ada over (her own `life` widens her home when something is in bloom) and his `social` need starts to fall. The letterbox's rule skips two days. Tests: `tests/test_life.py` (the same seed with and without the tin moved).
- **Told what to do** (geogen.101.8; the Sims' player loop): `npc.gd` keeps a queue of orders (`direct(option id)`, `choices(thing)`, `cancel_orders`, `_take_order`), each an option id done ahead of anything of its own by the chain `planner.plan_to(snapshot, goal)` finds to it (in `planner.py` and `npc_planner.gd`: the chain to a named affordance whether or not it is worth anything; trace `tests/data/planner/directed.json`, a trace step's `to:`). `_start(ranked)` is the shared tail of a decision (reserve, context, push). Orders use `options(false, true, told=true)`: no routine `only:`, no retry delay, and offers whatever the need. The HUD panel is `hud_direct.gd` (`GeogenHudDirect`, G), which owns the selection and the menu and tells `main.gd` by signal to change camera and mouse (`_direct_mode`, `_direct_look_at`, pan and zoom); it computes the menu on a pick, never on a refresh (`options()` draws from the NPC's rng). Details and the API in `runtime/godot/README.md` ("Directing people"); `tests/test_household_runtime.py`. Runtime tests that inject mouse events need the world running: a paused tree delivers no `_unhandled_input`.
- **Played as its people** (geogen.101.10): scene key `play: {as: people, households: [names]}` (composer `meta.play`, build-only; `export.py` writes the manifest's `play`; only the top scene's counts). In Godot `main._play_as` (deferred from `world_loaded`, so after the player is placed) parks the player and calls `GeogenHudDirect.set_playing`, which locks directing on, limits `people()` to the households and points `hud.actor` at the selection: panels already read through `hud.actor`, so that is all it takes for the hotbar, tracker and stats to be the selected person's. `GeogenNpc.free_will` (false: idle with no orders, outside a hold block) and `_orders` are in `save_state`. There are still ~55 literal `"player"` defaults in the Godot scripts (API and rules): they are right for a walking player and untouched; first-person possession of an NPC is not attempted. `tests/test_household_runtime.py`.
- **The lived-in cottage** (geogen.102): `scenes/cottage.yaml` param `lived_in` (default 1) makes the house 4 m wider to the east (its `house` slot moves 2 m so the west wall stays put; places along the front and back walls are measured for that) and adds two bedrooms there, a single bed each owned by Ana and Bea (`people/ana.yaml`, `people/bea.yaml`, with their own bedtimes), a bathroom partitioned off a corner (`partition_wall.yaml`, a plastered wall whose `front` / `back` take a door), a kitchen (fridge, sink, a 0.9 m range) and two householders, Ana and Bea (`household: cottage`), in place of the lone `resident` of `npcs/resident.yaml`; the lights-out quest is given by greeting Ana. Everything that nests the cottage (island, house_plot, town lots, `city.py`'s default) passes `lived_in: 0`, and so do the NPC, quest and save tests written around the one resident: fixture `plain_cottage` (tests/conftest.py) is that export, still named `cottage`. On `house.floor`, `facing: north` is into the room from the back wall (south faces the back wall).
- `examples/dollhouse/` (`--project examples/dollhouse -s lark_lane`, geogen.101.7): a street of four houses and nine people, none of them scripted. Three floor plans (`bungalow.yaml`, `family_house.yaml`, `share_house.yaml`) are placed with `furnish:` and filled by the solver from room types: the core `kitchen` type now has a range, fridge and sink as well as the table (appliances first, so they get the walls), and the project's `room_types/shared_kitchen.yaml` has a counter with a microwave instead of a range. Each person is a `kind: character` on the core `npcs/householder.yaml` (`people/*.yaml`: their own need curves, speed, and a night block `only: [bed], hold: true` at their own hours), placed with a `home: {rect}` covering their house only and `owns:` their bed. Walter's home also covers his front garden (a bench and `flower_bed.yaml`, whose affordance advertises `order` and `curiosity`). What was learned building it: (1) a 0.9 m door with a leaf between the two busiest rooms jams a shared house (two people meet head on, each one's far side of the door is the other, and a leaf someone else opened blocks paths along the wall), so living room to kitchen is `style: archway`, 1.6 m wide; bedrooms and bathrooms keep doors. (2) `owns:` by several NPCs is shared ownership (`npc.gd` `_owned_by_another` is false for an owner): a couple both own their double bed; without `owns:` two people take the two sides of a single bed. (3) A night block works with `planner:`: the block narrows the options to beds, a plan in hand is dropped (`replan`) and the bed is taken. (4) A plan claims a step when it reaches it (`_next_planned`), and from the start only a step that `sets` a state, which two people mustn't both do; each is given back as it finishes (`_finish_option`). So a housemate can use the range while the cook is still at the fridge, and the cook, finding it taken, plans again. (5) Bathrooms (geogen.101.9): the project's `room_types/bathroom.yaml` (an override) always has a bath and puts the toilet mid-wall with elbow room; the family house and the share house, 2 m wide, have `small_bathroom.yaml` (no bath, the toilet at the far end) because three people jam a narrow bathroom with a bath in it. A door's open leaf blocks the way along its wall, so the bungalow's bathroom door is far from its bedroom door and the share house's bathroom door opens away from the basin. The toilet's affordance (need `bladder`) is off in cubicles (`params: {in_stall: true}`, an affordance `when:`). (6) Everyone tops up needs that are barely down (seven or eight small meals a day): nothing else is worth more; fun and social needs (geogen.101.6) are what would fill the time. Like the other projects it has no hub portal (the hub is the core catalogue's). - Crowd cost (geogen.11.5.17, geogen.11.18): NPCs share one character grid per physics frame (`npc.gd` `_characters_in`), move waypoints off characters only over the next 8 m of path, and step every other frame when off screen and over 10 m from the player, or over 60 m away (`_throttled`). `-s crowd_test` (50 pedestrians, exhaustive tier) with runtime `--simulate=30 --npc-limit=N --disable-vsync` measures it: `npc cost:` gives per-tick callback time (`physics_ms`, p95), frame times, draw calls and primitives. ## Time of day

The Godot runtime has a world clock (`scripts/clock.gd`, `--time=HH:MM`, `--day-length=S`) that drives the sun, sky, moon and night state.
- **Lights:** `light: {..., auto: night}` fixtures (the rebuilt 4.5 m `street_lamp.yaml`, with an emissive `lamp_glass` lantern) turn on after dark, and vehicle lamps brighten.
- **NPC `routine:`:** blocks `{name, from, to, activities, preferences, away, at, only, hold}` are parsed by `npc.parse_routine`, with times as "HH:MM".
  - The active blocks bias scoring.
  - `away` runs the new `leave` action: `go_to: exit` (building entrance spawns), then `vanish: true` (a new step verb). The NPC reappears when the block ends.
  - `npcs/pedestrian.yaml` goes home 22:30–07:00, sits more at lunch, and strolls more in the evening.
  - `only: [tags]` (geogen.74.2) keeps a block's options to affordances with one of the tags (`owns`: the NPC's own things), none of its activities; `npc.gd` `options()` falls back to the unrestricted options when none is free. `hold: true` makes the affordance taken last until the block ends (`_hold_block`, ctx `held`: the `duration` wait, or a `play` looping on `duration`). `npcs/hotel_guest.yaml` sleeps 23:00–07:00; test scene `-s bedtime`, `tests/test_npc_runtime.py::test_a_guest_sleeps_the_night_in_bed`.
- **Traffic `schedule:`:** `[["HH:MM", share], ...]` parks surplus vehicles out of the player's sight. `traffic/town.yaml` drops to 15% at night.
- Details are in the runtime README's "Time of day" section.

## Emitters

A part's `emitter:` (particles) and `sound:` (a synthesised loop) are described physically, not in engine terms (`geogen/emitters.py`, whose header has the vocabulary): `rate`, `lifetime`, `speed`, `direction`, `spread`, `gravity` (m/s² downward, negative rises), `wind`, `drag`, `size` (birth, death), `color`, `opacity`, `material` (`droplet | soft | streak | glow`), `area` and `at` (an attachment point of the part or metres from its centre); sound is `band`, `hum`, `flutter`, `crackle`, `volume_db`, `range`. They export as `extras.geogen.emitter` / `.sound`; `world_emitters.gd` maps them to a `GPUParticles3D` and an `AudioStreamPlayer3D`, with sprites and audio generated at load (no files). A part with either gets a stable ID and a bool `running` (`state.emitter_state`, started from the block's `running:`) that switches both, like a fixture's `lit`: `bind: {nozzle.running: "self.valve == 'open'"}` or any rule. API: `world.emitters`. Offscreen renders and the Qt viewer don't draw particles. `-s emitter_yard` (test group) has a fountain (`pond.yaml`), chimney smoke (`chimney_stack.yaml`), a steam valve (`steam_vent.yaml`) and rain (`rain_cloud.yaml`); the survival campfire smokes, sparks and crackles while it burns.

## Lighting

Fixtures carry `meta.light` (`{type: omni, color, energy, range, offset: [x, y, z]}`): assets declare it with a top-level `light:` block (`table_lamp`, `floor_lamp`), ceiling pendants from finishes have one. Exports add a KHR_lights_punctual point light per fixture on a `<node>_light` child (intensity = energy × `CANDELA_PER_ENERGY`); the Godot runtime tunes those imported lights (energy, range, shadows) rather than adding its own, and `--lights` prints them. Every fixture that doesn't come on with the night has the rules state `lit` (bool, true; `state.light_state`), which the Godot runtime shows and hides its light and glow by (`WorldLoader._fixtures`), so any rule can turn a lamp off. Light switches have a `switch` interaction (on/off, rocker tips) and `meta.switch.light` (build-only): `quests.collect` compiles it to the binding `<fixture>#lit = <switch>#switch == "on"` (`state.switch_bindings`; a fixture with several switches, one by each of a room's doors, is switched multi-way: any of them toggles it). Floor plans add theirs (the same asset); elsewhere place `light_switch.yaml` (wall-mounted, `params: {light: <the fixture's placement name>}`; its asset key `switch: {light}` becomes `meta.switch`) and `ceiling_light.yaml` (a pendant that hangs below its origin). The fixture is found by `state.switched_light`: the nearest one of that name, so a scene placed twice keeps each copy's switches on its own lights. The cottage has two pendants and a reading lamp, each with a switch (on the house's `ceiling` and `*_inside` surfaces). The viewer's **Night** toggle (N, `--night`) dims the sun/sky and lights the scene with its fixtures (up to 15 nearest, range-windowed; from the uncut scene so cutaways stay lit; the ground ignores them since point lights have no shadows).

## Actions and motions

`assets/npcs/actions.yaml` steps can run motion files: `do: <motion>` with `roles`, `params` and `keep`, and `use:`, `pick_up:` and `put_down:` go through motions on a body with a rig (`docs/dev/characters.md`, "Actions run motions"). The state of an interaction changes when the motion's finger or hand arrives (`pressed`), not when the NPC arrives in front of it.

NPC actions and stimuli are layered: every content root's `npcs/actions.yaml` and `npcs/stimuli.yaml` are read, the core's first, so a project adds its own and replaces the ones it names again.
